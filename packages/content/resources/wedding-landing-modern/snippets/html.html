Our story

Two cities, one very long train ride, and a shared umbrella.

We met on a rainy platform in Milan in 2019 — one umbrella between two strangers heading the same way. Seven years, three apartments and one small dog named Pesto later, we're saying yes on the coast that started it all.

We would be honoured to have you with us for a weekend of good food, salt air and slightly too much dancing.
