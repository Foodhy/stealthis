## Policy Dashboard

A reassuring, single-screen home for a policyholder. The top row of stat cards sums up active
policies, total monthly premium, open claims and next renewal. Below, each active policy renders
as a rounded card with its type icon, premium, status badge and renewal date — policies inside the
renewal window pick up a pulsing **Renewing soon** badge.

A prominent next-payment panel shows the amount and due date with an inline **Pay now** flow that
confirms before marking the bill paid and updating the summary. Open claims list their stage on a
progress track, coverage limits render as at-a-glance usage bars, and a quick-actions row exposes
file-a-claim, pay and download-ID-card buttons. Everything is vanilla JS — no libraries.

> Illustrative UI only — not for real insurance, financial or legal use.
