The Story

An inheritance that comes due at midnight.

On the eve of her father's funeral, Eleanor Vance returns to the crumbling Vance estate to find the family fortune bound to a clause no one will explain. As guests arrive for the reading of the will, old loyalties fracture and a single hour decides three generations.

Adapted by award-winning playwright Mira Solano from the 1924 novel, The Gilded Hour moves with the precision of a clock and the heat of a confession — a production critics are calling the season's most assured revival.

  • Period Drama
  • Ages 12+
