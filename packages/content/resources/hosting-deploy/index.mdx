Where to ship it: **Vercel** for Next.js and frontends, **Cloudflare** for edge apps and Workers (plus D1),
**Netlify** for Jamstack sites, **Railway** and **Render** for backends with databases, **Fly.io** for
global low-latency VMs, and **AWS Amplify** when you're all-in on AWS.
