## Clinic Promo Video

A polished 9-second (270-frame) vertical promo designed for social media stories and healthcare marketing content. The composition opens on a deep dark background (`#0a1a18`) lit by a slow-breathing radial teal glow at centre. Scene 1 (0–2 s) springs the "Greenfield Medical Center" logotype and a white cross icon into view with a bounce spring, while a soft tagline fades up beneath. Scene 2 (2–5 s) introduces three teal service pills — Primary Care, Specialist, and Lab Tests — each sliding in from the left with a staggered 12-frame offset, creating a cascading reveal that guides the eye down the vertical frame.

Scene 3 (5–7.5 s) is the call-to-action block: the headline "Book Your Appointment Today" fades and scales in, followed by an animated coral underline that sweeps right-to-left from zero width. Below, the clinic phone number types in character by character at roughly one character per frame, building anticipation before the number is fully revealed. Scene 4 (7.5–9 s) closes with a teal horizontal gradient sweep that wipes across the frame, and the clinic name fades back in over the glow for a clean branded outro.

All configurable values — clinic name, tagline, phone number, service labels, and every color constant — live at the top of the file as `UPPERCASE` constants and the `SERVICES` array. Swapping them adapts the template to any medical practice in under a minute.

### Customization

- `CLINIC_NAME` — change to your practice name (also updates the outro fade)
- `CLINIC_TAGLINE` — sub-headline beneath the logo
- `CLINIC_PHONE` — phone number that types in during the CTA scene
- `SERVICES` — array of `{ icon, label }` objects for the three service pills
- `CTA_HEADLINE` — the large bold text in Scene 3
- Color constants `BG`, `TEAL`, `CORAL`, `MUTED` — remap to your brand palette
- Spring configs `{ damping: 14, stiffness: 120 }` on each entrance — lower `stiffness` for softer bounces
