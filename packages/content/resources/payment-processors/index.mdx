Which payment SDK to use depends heavily on **where your customers are**. Stripe wins on
developer experience globally, but local and regional processors often convert far better in
their market — Mercado Pago and dLocal in LatAm, Razorpay in India, Paystack and Flutterwave
in Africa, Mollie in Europe, Xendit in Southeast Asia, and Adyen/PayPal/Square worldwide. If
you sell software and want tax handled for you, a merchant of record like Paddle or Lemon
Squeezy is easiest. Tick the ones you care about in the comparison and match a processor to
each country you sell in.
