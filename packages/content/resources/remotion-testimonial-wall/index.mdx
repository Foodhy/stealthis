## Testimonial Wall Video

A broadcast-quality Remotion composition that presents six customer testimonials as an animated wall of cards on a dark SaaS canvas. Each card springs into view from a scaled-down state using spring physics, staggered so the grid fills naturally row by row. Every card shows an avatar circle with CSS-rendered initials, the reviewer's name, role, and company, a five-star rating row, and a two-line italic quote — all with realistic fictional product data from companies like Launchpad, Veritas Cloud, and Nexus AI.

Once all six cards are visible, a sequential highlight sweep plays through each card one at a time: the spotlighted card scales up slightly, its border glows with the brand indigo accent, and a subtle top-edge radial shimmer fires. This draws the viewer's eye across every testimonial without requiring them to read simultaneously. The remaining cards dim gently during each spotlight so focus is unambiguous.

The composition closes with a rating summary section that animates up from below: five gold stars, a large counter that counts up from 4.0 to 4.9, and a review count that climbs from 1,800 to 2,400+. Use this composition in product launch campaigns, landing-page hero loops, conference slide decks, or batch-rendered social proof reels with swapped review data.
