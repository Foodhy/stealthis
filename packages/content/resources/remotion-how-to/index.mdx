## How-To Step Video

A sequential how-to video that reveals four numbered steps one at a time. Each step card slides in from the left, shows a circled step number in the accent color, a bold step title, and a short description line. After all steps are revealed, a "Done!" badge pulses onto the screen. A dotted vertical connector line between steps draws itself progressively.

### Composition specs

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1280 × 720 |
| FPS | 30 |
| Duration | 7 s (210 frames) |

### Usage

Copy `react.tsx` into your Remotion project, import `RemotionRoot` in your `Root.tsx`, and run `npx remotion studio` to preview.
