## Post-Meal Feedback Form

Multi-step form: Step 1 — emoji mood scale (😞😐🙂😊🤩) for overall experience; Step 2 — star ratings (1–5) for Food quality, Service, Ambiance, Value; Step 3 — optional free-text "Tell us more" + a checkbox "May we contact you?"; Step 4 — thank you screen with summary of their ratings. Progress bar across top.
