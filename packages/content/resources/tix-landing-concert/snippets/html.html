FAQ

Everything you need before doors.

How will I receive my tickets?

All tickets are mobile-only. A QR code drops to your account 48 hours before doors — screenshots won't scan, so bring the live pass.

What's the ticket limit?

Four tickets per person per show. Orders over the limit are auto-cancelled to keep bots off the floor.

Can I resell a ticket I can't use?

Yes — only through verified resale inside your account. Off-platform tickets cannot be scanned at the gate.

Are the shows all-ages?

All ages welcome. Under-16s must be with an adult in seated tiers. Front Pit is 16+ for safety.

What can I bring in?

Clear bags up to 12"×6"×12", a sealed water bottle, and your energy. No pro cameras, no outside food.

Don't watch the clips.
Be in them.

Floors are moving fast across the run. Lock your night before it flips to sold out.
