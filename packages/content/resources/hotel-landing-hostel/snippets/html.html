Our story

Born from a backpack & a bold idea

Basecamp started in 2019 when three friends returned from a year of couch-surfing across Asia and realised hostels didn't have to be dingy. We gutted a 1920s Alfama townhouse, kept the original tiles, added solar panels, and built a place we'd actually want to stay.

Five years and 40,000 guests later, Basecamp is rated the #1 hostel in Lisbon on Hostelworld. We're proud of it. Come stay.
