About the role

Northwind Labs builds tooling that helps climate teams turn raw sensor data into decisions. As our Senior Product Designer you'll own the end-to-end experience of our analytics suite — from first sketch to shipped flow — partnering tightly with research, product and a six-person engineering pod.

You'll set the visual and interaction language for a product used daily by scientists who care more about clarity than decoration. This is a high-ownership role for someone who enjoys ambiguity and ships at a steady, deliberate pace.

What you'll do

  • Lead design for two product areas, from discovery through high-fidelity delivery.
  • Run lightweight research sessions and translate findings into crisp design decisions.
  • Grow and maintain our component library alongside front-end engineers.
  • Pair with PMs to scope work into small, shippable increments.
  • Raise the craft bar across the team through critique and mentorship.

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years designing complex web applications, ideally data-heavy products.
  • A portfolio that shows systems thinking, not just polished screens.
  • Fluency in Figma and comfort reading the front-end you hand off.
  • Strong written communication — much of the team works async.
  • Bonus: experience with charts, maps or scientific visualisation.

Benefits & perks

  • 💻 €1,500 home-office budget
  • 📚 Yearly learning stipend
  • 🌍 4 weeks work-from-anywhere
  • 📈 Equity options
