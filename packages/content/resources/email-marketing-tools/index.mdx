resets and receipts see [Email & SMS Providers](/r/email-sms-providers). **Loops** for SaaS onboarding
and product updates. **Mailchimp** for classic newsletters. **Brevo** for email + SMS + CRM in one.
**ConvertKit** for creators and paid newsletters.
