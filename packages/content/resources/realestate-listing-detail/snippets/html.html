About this home

Set behind a low brass-tipped gate on one of Caldera Heights' most coveted streets, the Calderwood Residence pairs 1929 Spanish-Colonial bones with a quietly modern renovation. Hand-troweled plaster, reclaimed oak floors, and steel-framed windows frame a sun-washed great room that opens onto a walled garden with mature olive trees.

The chef's kitchen anchors the main level with honed marble, a paneled refrigerator, and a breakfast banquette. Upstairs, the primary suite reads like a private retreat — vaulted ceilings, a dressing room, and a spa bath clad in zellige tile. A detached studio over the two-car garage offers flexible space for a home office or guest quarters.
