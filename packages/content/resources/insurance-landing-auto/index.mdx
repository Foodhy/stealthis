## Auto Insurance Landing

A confident, trust-forward landing page for an auto-insurance brand. The hero pairs a big savings
claim with an inline quick-quote widget — enter a ZIP, vehicle year and coverage level and it
estimates a friendly monthly premium on the spot, no page reload.

Below the fold sit coverage-type cards (liability, collision, comprehensive, roadside), a why-us
benefits row, discount badges for safe drivers and bundling, real-sounding testimonials, and a
mobile-app claims CTA. The palette is calm blue with a trust-green accent, rounded cards and big
confident numbers throughout.

Everything is vanilla HTML, CSS and JS — the quote estimator and discount toggles are wired with a
few small event listeners and no libraries.

> Illustrative UI only — not a real insurance product, quote or offer.
