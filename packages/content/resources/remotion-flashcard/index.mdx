## Flashcard Flip Video

A flashcard animation that shows three flashcards cycling through with a flip animation. Each card occupies full frame centered. The front side (question) is a dark indigo card; the flip mid-point triggers a color change to a lighter card showing the answer. After the answer shows, checkmarks appear and the card slides out while the next slides in.

### Composition specs

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1280 × 720 |
| FPS | 30 |
| Duration | 8 s (240 frames) |

### Usage

Copy `react.tsx` into your Remotion project, import `RemotionRoot` in your `Root.tsx`, and run `npx remotion studio` to preview.
