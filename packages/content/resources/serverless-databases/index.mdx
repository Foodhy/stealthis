This topic is **databases only** — not full backends. **Neon** is the go-to for serverless Postgres with
branching. **PlanetScale** covers MySQL at scale. **Turso** and **Cloudflare D1** put SQLite at the edge.
**CockroachDB Serverless** for globally distributed Postgres-compatible SQL. **Supabase** bundles Postgres
with auth and storage — see [Backend as a Service](/r/backend-as-a-service) for that comparison.
