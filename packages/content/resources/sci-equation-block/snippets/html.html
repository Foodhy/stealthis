A Reduced Two-Box Model for Coupled Climate–Ocean Heat Transport

E. Marchetti1, R. Okonkwo2, and L. Vance1 1Institute for Climate Dynamics, Aurelia University  ·  2Marine Systems Lab, Cape Verde Polytechnic

Open Access Peer Reviewed DOI: 10.4271/jgt.2026.0483

Abstract

We present a reduced-order energy-balance model in which the surface and deep ocean are represented as two well-mixed reservoirs exchanging heat through a diffusive interface. The governing equations admit a closed-form equilibrium that we use to estimate the effective climate sensitivity from the synthetic Helios Drift dataset.
