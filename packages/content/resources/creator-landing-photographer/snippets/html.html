About

Photographs that feel like the room they were taken in.

For twelve years I’ve chased the kind of light you only notice when you slow down. My work lives between editorial precision and documentary honesty — nothing staged into stiffness, nothing left to luck.

I shoot on a mix of medium-format film and digital, and I edit every frame by hand. Whether it’s a founder’s headshot or a three-day wedding, the brief is the same: make it true, make it last.
