04 / SAFETY & TRAINING

No badge, no run. Every time.

Machines stay locked until you've passed the sign-off. Two safety officers are on the floor during staffed hours, and every bay has an e-stop within reach.

  • ① Mandatory floor induction before first run
  • ② Per-machine badge with practical sign-off
  • ③ PPE wall: glasses, gloves, hearing, respirators
  • ④ Logged runs & buddy rule on CNC / laser
