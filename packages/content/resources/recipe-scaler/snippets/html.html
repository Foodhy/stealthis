Charred Tomato & Saffron Orzo

    Method

    1. 1

      Toast the orzo in olive oil over medium heat until nutty and golden, about 3 minutes.

    2. 2

      Add the cherry tomatoes and garlic; let them blister and burst, pressing a few with a spoon.

    3. 3

      Bloom the saffron in the warm stock, pour in, season, and simmer until the orzo is tender and creamy.

    4. 4

      Off the heat, fold through butter and a squeeze of lemon. Finish with torn basil and shaved cheese.
