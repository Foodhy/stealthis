Pick by how much infrastructure you want to run. **Inngest** and **Trigger.dev** are the modern serverless picks
for TypeScript apps — events, retries, and steps without managing Redis. **BullMQ** is the self-hosted classic
when you already run Redis and want full queue control. **Temporal** is for durable, long-running workflows
where guaranteed completion matters more than simplicity.
