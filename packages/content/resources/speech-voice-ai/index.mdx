Building voice features? For **text-to-speech**, Kokoro and Pocket TTS are tiny and run
locally, F5-TTS and Zonos excel at voice cloning, VibeVoice handles long multi-speaker
audio, and Higgs Audio covers many languages. For **speech-to-text**, whisper.cpp
transcribes on-device. Compare by task, strength, and license (mind the non-commercial ones).
