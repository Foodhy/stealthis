When to come in right away

If your pet shows any of these signs, call us or head over — do not wait until morning.

  • 🫁 Trouble breathing or choking
  • 🩸 Bleeding that will not stop
  • 🚗 Hit by a car or a bad fall
  • 🤢 Repeated vomiting or diarrhea
  • ⚡ Seizures or sudden collapse
  • 🧪 Ate something toxic (chocolate, xylitol, meds)
  • 😣 Bloated, hard belly or can't pee
  • 🌡️ Heatstroke or won't stand up
