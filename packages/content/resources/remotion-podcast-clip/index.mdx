## Podcast Clip Template

A full 60-second horizontal podcast clip template for repurposing long-form episodes into shareable social content. The layout features a two-column design: a speaker panel on the left with an animated avatar ring glow and podcast logo, and a content panel on the right with a live waveform, episode title, and a quote that reveals itself word-by-word. A persistent top bar shows the podcast name and episode number, while a bottom bar animates a progress bar alongside a running timestamp. Everything is rendered with Remotion — no external assets needed — making it easy to drop in real episode data and render directly to MP4.

## Composition specs

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1920 × 1080 |
| FPS | 30 |
| Duration | 60 s (1800 frames) |

## Elements

- **Top bar** — podcast name ("The Dev Dispatch") and episode label (Ep. 142 · "Building in Public") on a semi-transparent dark strip
- **Left panel (40%)** — circular avatar placeholder with a pulsing ring-glow halo, speaker name below, podcast logo initial above
- **Right panel (60%)** — 20-bar animated waveform with sine-wave height offsets per bar and frame; episode title; quote text that fades in word by word over the first 15 seconds
- **Bottom bar** — full-width progress bar tracking playhead position, with elapsed timestamp ("0:12") and total duration ("1:02:34") labels
- **Background** — dark gradient from `#0d1117` to `#161b22` with a subtle radial accent glow
