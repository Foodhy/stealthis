The models and gateways worth defaulting to. Reach for a proprietary frontier family
(Claude, GPT, Gemini) when you need peak capability, an **open-weight** family (Llama, Qwen,
DeepSeek, Mistral) when you want to self-host or fine-tune, or a gateway like OpenRouter
when you want to compare providers and add fallbacks without rewriting integrations.
