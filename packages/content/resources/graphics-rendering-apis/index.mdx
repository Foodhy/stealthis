The layer underneath every engine. Go low-level with **Vulkan**, **DirectX 12**, or **Metal**
for maximum control; learn or stay portable with **OpenGL**; target the web with **WebGL**
(today) or **WebGPU** (the modern successor). Don't want the boilerplate? **raylib** is the
friendliest start, and **SDL** handles windowing/input under almost everything. Compare by
how low-level they are and which platforms they target.
