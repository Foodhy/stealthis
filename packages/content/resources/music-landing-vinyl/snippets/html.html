Why analog

Three reasons we never went digital

Warmth

Analog tape rounds the highs and fattens the lows. You don’t just hear the record — you feel the room it was cut in.

Ritual

Sleeve in hand, needle down, side over. A deliberate forty minutes that asks you to actually listen instead of skip.

Artwork

Twelve inches of cover art, liner notes and inner sleeves. The album as an object you can hold, frame and pass on.
