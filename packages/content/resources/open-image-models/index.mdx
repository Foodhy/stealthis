For builders who want to **run or fine-tune** image generation rather than use a consumer
app: Stability AI and FLUX are strong open base models, while Qwen-Image-Edit LoRAs add
specific powers like multi-angle re-rendering (Multiple Angles) and reference-based posing
(AnyPose). Several have live Hugging Face demos — try before you self-host.
