When your product **sends** webhooks to customers, **Svix** handles delivery, signatures, and
retries. When you **receive** webhooks from Stripe or Shopify, **Hookdeck** adds routing, replay,
and reliability — pair with [Payment Processors](/r/payment-processors). **Convoy** for
open-source or self-hosted webhook infrastructure. **EventBridge** when you're already on AWS
and need an event bus between services. For async work triggered by events, see
[Background Jobs](/r/background-jobs).
