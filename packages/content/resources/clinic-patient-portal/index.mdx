## Patient Portal Dashboard

The home a patient lands on after signing in. A calm, clinical-white layout in teal and soft
coral keeps the next visit front and centre: a hero card shows the upcoming appointment with a
**live countdown**, plus check-in and join-video actions that update in place. A quick-actions
grid covers the common jobs — book, refill, message, view results. Below, recent lab results
appear as tiles with normal / review / pending status badges, active prescriptions list with
one-tap refill requests, and the care team is shown as an avatar strip. All interactions are
vanilla JS.

> Illustrative UI only — **not** intended for real medical use.
