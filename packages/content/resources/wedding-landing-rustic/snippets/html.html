Our Story

Two city planners who met over a shared table at a countryside market and never stopped building things together.

2019

The Farmers' Market

Amelia reached for the last jar of wildflower honey. Rowan reached at the same moment. They split it, shared a bench, and talked until the stalls packed up.

2022

The Old Farmhouse

They restored a leaning porch on a rented plot outside town, learning that a good partnership is mostly patience, coffee, and a level.

2025

The Proposal

Under a string of café lights at the same barn where they'll marry, Rowan asked. Amelia said yes before he finished the sentence.
