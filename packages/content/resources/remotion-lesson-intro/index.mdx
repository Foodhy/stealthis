## Lesson Intro Animation

A lesson intro composition: animated lesson number badge (e.g. "Lesson 03") springs in from left, course name fades in above it, lesson title does a large bold reveal, and an accent underline draws itself beneath the title. A subtle animated dot grid in the background provides depth without distraction.
