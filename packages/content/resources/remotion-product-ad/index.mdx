## Product Ad Spot

A punchy 5-second commercial spot for a wireless earbud product rendered entirely with Remotion. The composition is split into two columns: the left holds an animated product hero card with a glint-sweep and gentle float, while the right column builds up the sales copy layer by layer — subtitle first, then a letter-by-letter product name, a slide-in divider, three staggered benefit items, a spring-popped price badge, and finally a shimmer CTA button.

Every element uses `spring()` for entrance so nothing ever feels mechanical. The price badge adds a continuous pulse-ring animation after it springs into place, and the CTA button runs a repeating shimmer sweep to draw the eye. A radial ambient glow with a subtle dot-grid background gives the scene cinematic depth without competing with the foreground content. The final 20 frames fade the entire composition to black.

Customization is straightforward: swap `PRODUCT_NAME`, `PRODUCT_SUBTITLE`, `BENEFITS`, `PRICE`, and `PRICE_ORIGINAL` constants at the top of the file, and change `BRAND_COLOR` / `BRAND_GLOW` to match any brand palette.
