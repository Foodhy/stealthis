Questions, answered plainly

Do I need a medical exam?

Most applicants up to $1,000,000 in coverage qualify with no exam — just a few health questions answered online. Larger policies may request a quick visit.

What's the difference between term and whole?

Term covers you for a fixed period (10–30 years) at the lowest cost. Whole covers you for life and builds cash value you can borrow against. You can convert term to whole later with no new exam.

How fast are claims paid?

Once we receive the required documents, the median claim is paid in nine days. In 2025 we approved 99.2% of claims submitted.

Can I change my beneficiaries later?

Yes — add, remove or re-weight beneficiaries anytime from your online account. Changes take effect immediately.

What if I cancel?

You can cancel anytime with no penalty. There's also a 30-day money-back window if you change your mind after starting a policy.
