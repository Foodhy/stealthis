This topic is **public status communication** — not error tracking or log search. **Better Stack**
ties status pages to uptime checks and incidents (also listed under
[Logging Platforms](/r/logging-platforms)). **Instatus** for a polished, standalone status site.
**Statuspage** for enterprise teams in the Atlassian stack. **Cachet** when you want open-source
pages you host yourself. For crash reporting and session replay, see
[Error Monitoring](/r/error-monitoring).
