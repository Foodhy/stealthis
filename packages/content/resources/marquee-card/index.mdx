## Marquee Card

A compact card with a ticker along the top edge. The track is rendered twice and translated by `-50%` so the seam is invisible.

### How it works

`.marquee__track` is `display: flex` with two identical `.marquee__group` children. The animation moves the track from `0` to `-50%`. Hover sets `animation-play-state: paused`.

### When to use it

- Status strips on product cards
- Event or release tickers
- Short repeating labels, not long paragraphs
