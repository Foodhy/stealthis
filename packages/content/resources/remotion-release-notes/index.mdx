## Release Notes Reel

A product changelog brought to life as a Remotion video. The composition covers three consecutive version releases — v3.4.0, v3.3.2, and v3.3.0 — each rendered on a dark document-style surface card with a left accent stripe. Every 4 seconds a new version block enters from below, driven by a spring with tuned damping so the card settles without bouncing. The version badge scales in with a pop, the release date fades in a beat later, and the four bullet items stagger left-to-right — 18 frames apart — giving viewers just enough time to read each line before the next arrives.

Bullet items carry semantic type indicators: a filled star (✦) for new features, an up-arrow (↑) for improvements, and a cross (✕) for bug fixes. Each icon sits inside a tinted pill matched to a status color — emerald for new, amber for improved, rose for fixed — providing instant visual scanning without needing to read the label text. After the last item settles, a thin gradient divider scales in horizontally to separate the block from the one that follows. The composition is capped by a 3-pixel progress bar at the top edge that fills from left to right across the full 12 seconds, giving the video a polished broadcast quality.

Use this composition in product update emails, in-app changelog modals, social teasers for release announcements, or embedded in SaaS onboarding sequences. Swap the `RELEASES` constant array at the top of `react.tsx` with your own version data — the layout scales automatically to any item count.
