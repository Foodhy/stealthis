## Album Release Promo

A three-act social-media promo for an album release, rendered in Remotion at 1920×1080 and 30 fps for a total of 180 frames (6 seconds). The composition is fully self-contained — every visual, from the abstract cover artwork to the rotating vinyl grooves, is generated with CSS gradients and inline SVG-style div layouts. No image assets or external libraries are required beyond `remotion` and React.

**Act 1 (frames 0–40)** opens on a near-black canvas with a deep indigo radial bloom. The album title "NEON SILENCE" materialises letter-by-letter: each character interpolates its opacity and Y-position with a three-frame stagger and a subtle purple glow burst that fades once the letter has settled. The artist name fades in below once the last character is revealed, giving the intro a clean typographic rhythm.

**Act 2 (frames 40–140)** is the main content act. A square album cover — built from a conic gradient and concentric rings with a gold-glowing center orb — springs in from 0.3× to 1× scale using `spring()` with a gentle overshoot. A vinyl record disc rendered entirely from `div` layers (groove rings, specular highlight, branded center label) sits alongside the cover and rotates continuously at two degrees per frame. To the right of a glowing vertical divider, eight track titles slide in from the right with a six-frame stagger, the title track highlighted in purple. The whole act cross-fades to black in the final four frames.

**Act 3 (frames 140–180)** snaps in after a brief black-flash transition. The artist name and album title fade up, then "OUT NOW" springs from 0× to 1× with a high-stiffness `spring()` producing a crisp pop. The text is rendered in gold with a multi-layer pulsing `textShadow` that breathes with a `Math.sin` oscillation. A slim gold rule and the streaming platform list fade up below, completing the release call-to-action.

> Simulated audio data — waveform values are generated mathematically. No real audio file is required.
