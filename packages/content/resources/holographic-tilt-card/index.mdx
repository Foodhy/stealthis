## Holographic Tilt Card

A collectible-style card that tilts toward the pointer and paints a rainbow foil glare from the same coordinates.

### How it works

1. `pointermove` maps the cursor to `--px` / `--py` (0–100%) and to `rotateX` / `rotateY`.
2. A foil layer uses those percentages in a `radial-gradient` plus a conic rainbow, blended with `color-dodge`.
3. `pointerleave` eases the card back to a flat pose.

### When to use it

- Product or collectible showcases
- Feature tiles that should feel physical
- Dark backgrounds where the foil reads clearly
