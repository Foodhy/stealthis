## Breaking News Bumper

This Remotion composition delivers a broadcast-quality breaking news bumper across four scenes at 1280×720, 30 fps. It opens with a bold diagonal red band that sweeps across the frame at high speed — skewed at 8 degrees with a glow shadow — revealing the words "BREAKING NEWS" in 56px heavy type. The band exits to the right by frame 22, leaving behind a dark charcoal canvas ready for the main card.

The main card (frames 20–80) builds layer by layer. A 12px solid red border bar scales up on the left edge, then the headline springs in from the left using a spring with `damping: 22, stiffness: 160` — heavy enough to land with authority without bounce. The 48px white headline and 26px subhead fade up separately with slight Y offsets, while the NNX network logo (bold red circle + text) pops into the top-right corner via a tight `damping: 18, stiffness: 200` spring. A faint background grid and radial red glow add broadcast depth.

From frame 80, the bottom chyron bar slides up from below. It has a dark-red-to-charcoal gradient, a "LIVE" badge with a blinking red dot (alternating every 20 frames), a magnitude badge, and a continuously scrolling location text strip. By frame 120, the chyron slides back down and the outro begins: a dark vertical wipe sweeps left-to-right clearing the frame, followed by a large centered NNX stamp that springs into view and fades out by frame 150.

### Customization

- `NETWORK_NAME` — channel call letters shown in the logo circle and outro stamp (default: NNX)
- `HEADLINE` — main headline text in 48px heavy white type (default: MAJOR EARTHQUAKE STRIKES PACIFIC COAST)
- `SUBHEAD` — secondary line below the headline (default: Rescue efforts underway — 3.2M affected)
- `LOCATION_TEXT` — scrolling chyron content repeated across the bottom bar
- `MAGNITUDE` — numeric magnitude displayed in the chyron badge (default: 7.8)
- `LOCAL_TIME` — time string shown in the network logo area (default: 14:32)
- `ACCENT_RED` — primary red accent color used for the band, border bar, glow, and chyron (default: #e8001e)
- `BG` — main background color (default: #0d1117)
