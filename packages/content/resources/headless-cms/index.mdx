**Sanity** is the developer favorite for flexible, real-time structured content. **Payload** embeds in
Next.js. **Strapi** and **Keystone** for open-source self-hosting. **Hygraph** when GraphQL is your API.
**TinaCMS** for Git-backed MDX with visual editing. **Contentful** for enterprise localization and SLAs.
For translation workflows, see [Localization Platforms](/r/localization-platforms).
