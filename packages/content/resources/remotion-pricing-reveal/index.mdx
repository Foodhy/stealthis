## Pricing Reveal Animation

A 10-second Remotion composition that animates a three-tier SaaS pricing scene on a dark cinematic background. The sequence opens with the "Flowbase — Simple Pricing" header sliding down into frame, then each card (Starter, Pro, Enterprise) launches upward in a staggered spring sequence 22 frames apart. The center Pro card is visually elevated — slightly taller and wider, framed by a persistent indigo glow and a "Most Popular" badge that pulses with a breathing scale animation throughout the composition.

Inside each card, four or five feature rows enter from the left one by one using individual spring curves, landing in a clean staggered cascade. Once all features are revealed, the CTA button rises from below and fades in. At frame 175, after all three cards have fully entered, the Pro card fires a quick scale-pop to draw the viewer's eye back to the recommended tier. The composition closes around frame 240 with a "14-day free trial — no credit card required" tagline fading up from below, then everything gently fades to black over the final 15 frames.

The layout is pure React with inline styles only — no external CSS, no fonts, no assets. A subtle dot-grid overlay and two large radial blurs (indigo overhead, cyan bottom-right) give depth to the dark background without competing with the card content.
