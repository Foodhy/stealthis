Frequently asked

What's the refund policy?+

All sales are final, but tickets are 100% transferable up to 24 hours before doors via your order page.

Is it all ages?+

The festival is 18+. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID — wristbands are issued at the gate.

Can I bring a bag?+

Clear bags up to 12"×6"×12" are allowed. No outside food, glass, or professional cameras.

What if it rains?+

The event is rain or shine. Most stages are covered; ponchos are sold on-site.
