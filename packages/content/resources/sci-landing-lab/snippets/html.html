Focus areas

Four threads, one question: how do molecules move?

01 · Protein dynamics

Long-timescale folding & allostery

We accelerate molecular-dynamics sampling with learned collective variables, recovering millisecond conformational transitions from microsecond trajectories. Models are validated against HDX-MS and single-molecule FRET data.

  • Enhanced sampling
  • Markov state models
  • Allostery

02 · Ligand discovery

Generative design of binders

Diffusion models propose novel small-molecule scaffolds conditioned on pocket geometry, then rank them with a free-energy surrogate. Top candidates enter our collaborators' wet-lab assay pipeline within days.

  • Diffusion models
  • Free-energy
  • Docking

03 · Membrane systems

Ion channels at the lipid interface

Multiscale models couple atomistic channel cores to continuum membranes, predicting gating kinetics and conductance under physiological gradients. Equation of motion:

m r̈ = −∇U(r) − γṙ + √(2γkBT) ξ(t) (1)

04 · Methods & tools

Open, reproducible software

We release every model and pipeline as documented, tested open source. Our helixmd toolkit powers the lab and a growing external community of computational structural biologists.

  • Open source
  • Reproducibility
  • Benchmarks
