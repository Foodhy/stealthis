Chronicles of the Realm

The Lore of Ardennfell

Unfurl each scroll to read the chapters of a dying age.

I The Sundering of the Ember Throne ▾

When the High King fell to his own crown, the Ember that warmed all Ardennfell guttered low. Nine houses tore the realm asunder, each claiming the right to relight the throne — and each, in turn, was swallowed by the cold they could not master.

II Rise of the Hollow Reign ▾

From the unlit places came the Hollow — wraiths of every soul left to freeze in the dark. They do not hunger for flesh, but for warmth, and where they pass the very memory of fire is unmade.

III The Oath of the Vanguard ▾

A handful of wanderers swore the old vow at the Ashen Cairn: to carry the last true flame across the ruined leagues to the Ember Throne, though every league should cost a life. They are the Vanguard, and you are sworn among them.
