## Birthday Greeting Video

A festive, fully animated birthday greeting video rendered with Remotion at 30 fps. Every element enters with a carefully tuned spring or interpolate animation so the result feels playful and alive rather than mechanical. The warm background blends soft yellows, pinks, and lavenders while subtle polka dots add texture without visual noise.

The composition is split into six independent sub-components — `ConfettiBurst`, `BalloonLayer`, `SparkleLayer`, `HappyBirthdayText`, `NameLine`, `AgeBadge`, and `WishesRibbon` — each managing its own timing offset. Confetti particles use a deterministic seeded-random generator so the burst looks organic yet reproducible across renders. Balloons are individually animated with a sine-wave horizontal wobble and a gravity-like ease-out ascent. Simply change `BIRTHDAY_NAME` and `BIRTHDAY_AGE` at the top of the file to personalise the card.

The color palette draws from bright yellow, hot pink, purple, orange, sky blue, and green — all layered with subtle radial glows and drop shadows for visual depth. No external packages or assets are required beyond `react` and `remotion`.
