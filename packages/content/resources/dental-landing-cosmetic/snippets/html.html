    Do veneers damage my natural teeth?

    Modern porcelain veneers need only minimal enamel preparation, and no-prep options exist for suitable cases. We always plan conservatively.

    How long does whitening last?

    Typically 6–12 months depending on diet and habits. We include a top-up tray so you can refresh at home whenever you like.

    Will my new smile look natural?
