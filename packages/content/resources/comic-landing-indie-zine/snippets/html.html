hi, i'm Pell.

i draw Smudge & Staple at my kitchen table with a leaky brush pen and a photocopier i rescued from a curb. it's about two ghosts, Mop & Bracket, who are stuck doing the chores they ignored while alive.

no studio. no ads. just me, riso orange #ff4d00, and one very tolerant cat. new strips go up whenever the toner cooperates.

— Pell, somewhere damp
