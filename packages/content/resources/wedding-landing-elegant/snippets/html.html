Our Story

How two paths became one

✦
  1. 2019

    A rainy bookshop

    We reached for the same worn copy of a poetry collection in a little shop in Trastevere. Neither of us let go — so we shared a coffee instead.

  2. 2021

    The long way home

    Two cities, endless letters, and a shared calendar of countdowns. Distance taught us how much we wanted the same horizon.

  3. 2024

    A quiet yes

    On a cliffside at dusk, with the sea below and no one watching, Elliot asked and Amara laughed before she said yes.

  4. 2026

    Forever, out loud

    Now we gather everyone we love to celebrate the beginning of the rest of it. We would be honored to have you there.
