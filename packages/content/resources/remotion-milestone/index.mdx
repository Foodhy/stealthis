## Milestone Celebration

A full-screen celebration animation for sharing big numbers — subscriber counts, user milestones, order totals, or any achievement worth commemorating. The number springs in at frame 15 with an overshoot (`damping: 11, stiffness: 160`) for an energetic pop, then five concentric rings expand outward from the center at staggered delays, each easing out via a cubic curve before fading. Sixty confetti dots burst from the same origin point using per-index deterministic trajectories (no `Math.random()`) with gravity applied each frame, staying reproducible across renders.

Color palette is teal/emerald on near-black (`#060e0d`), giving the bright greens strong contrast without feeling harsh. A subtle dot-grid overlay reinforces depth. At frame 88 a branded end card lifts in with a logo tile, brand name, and a "Thank You!" line — easily customised by editing the `BRAND_NAME` and `MILESTONE_LABEL` constants at the top of the file.

Customize `MILESTONE_NUMBER`, `MILESTONE_LABEL`, and `BRAND_NAME` to match your campaign. Swap `TEAL`/`EMERALD` for your brand palette if needed.
