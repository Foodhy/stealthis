The Path

From white belt to fight team

A clear progression so you always know what you're working toward — no guesswork, just earned rank.

  1. 01

    Foundations

    Stance, breathing, basic strikes and gym etiquette. Build a habit and a body that can train.

  2. 02

    Fundamentals

    Combinations, defense and live drilling at controlled intensity. Earn your first stripe.

  3. 03

    Sparring Ready

    Controlled sparring and rolling with intent. Read distance, manage pace, stay composed.

  4. 04

    Fight Team

    Invitation-only competition squad. Camps, corner work and a shot at the amateur circuit.
