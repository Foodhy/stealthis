About

I design parts that earn their filament. Most of what I publish started as something broken in my own workshop — a bracket that snapped, a drawer that swallowed screwdrivers, a printer that needed one more sensor mount than the vendor shipped.

Every model is released with a printed-and-verified profile, tolerance notes and, where it matters, the parametric source file. If a part needs a specific clearance to work, that number is in the description, not hidden in a comment thread.
