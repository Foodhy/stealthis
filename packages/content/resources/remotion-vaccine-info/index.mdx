## Vaccine Info Clip

A polished 8-second (240-frame) vertical vaccine awareness clip designed for healthcare social media stories and waiting-room display loops. The composition opens with the dark `#0a1a18` clinic background lit by a soft breathing radial glow. A title bar — "💉 Stay Protected — Vaccination Guide" — springs down from above with a bounce entrance and a teal accent line beneath it, establishing the educational framing before the first vaccine panel appears.

Three vaccine info panels fill the middle 210 frames, each inside a `Sequence` of 70 frames. Panel 1 covers the annual flu vaccine with a teal syringe SVG icon; Panel 2 presents the COVID-19 Booster with a coral shield SVG; Panel 3 details the Hepatitis B three-dose series with a green circular-check SVG. Every panel slides up from 80 px below with a spring entrance and carries a title, sub-label, and a descriptive detail line. The background radial glow recolors per panel — teal → coral → green — creating an unmistakable visual cue for each vaccine category. A final CTA frame fades in at frame 210 with "Book your vaccination at Greenfield Medical Center" and a pulsing teal button.

All configurable values — clinic name, vaccine titles, detail lines, and every color constant — are declared as `UPPERCASE` constants and the `VACCINES` array at the top of the file. Swapping them adapts the clip to any vaccine schedule in under a minute without touching animation logic.

### Customization

- `CLINIC_NAME` — replace with your facility name (appears in the CTA frame)
- `CTA_LABEL` — change the bottom button text (e.g. `"Call to Schedule"`)
- `TITLE_TEXT` — the top heading (default: `"Stay Protected — Vaccination Guide"`)
- `VACCINES` array — each entry has `name`, `schedule`, `detail`, `iconType` (`"syringe"` | `"shield"` | `"check"`), and `accent` color
- Spring config `SP = { damping: 14, stiffness: 120 }` — lower `stiffness` for softer panel entrances
- `GLOW_RADIUS` — size of the per-panel background radial glow (default: `700`)
