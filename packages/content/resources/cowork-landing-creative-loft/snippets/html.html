Community of makers

You'll borrow a clamp and leave with a collaborator.

Foundry Loft runs on the small generosities of people who share walls: a borrowed lens, a second opinion on a logo, a critique over cold brew. Wednesday crits, Friday show-and-tell, and a members' wall that's never empty.

  • Weekly crits — open desk reviews, no ego required
  • Skill swaps — trade a retouch for a typeface
  • Open studios — quarterly public nights with sales
  • Members' board — gigs, gear and gallery calls
