About the author

I grew up on the edge of a tide.

Eleanor Vane was born in a fishing town where the weather wrote the calendar. She studied marine biology before abandoning the lab for the page, and her fiction still carries that close, patient attention to the natural world.

Her debut, The Quiet Hours, was a word-of-mouth phenomenon; Winter Lantern followed two years later to a Booker longlisting. She lives between a flat in the city and a draughty cottage by the harbour, where she writes longhand at a kitchen table that has seen four books and one very persistent cat.

"I'm not interested in plot for its own sake. I'm interested in the moment the kettle clicks off and someone finally says the true thing."
