From EP 148

Key takeaways in 90 seconds

01

Raise prices before you add features — willingness to pay is a signal you already have.

02

Segment by outcome, not by seat count. Buyers pay for the result, not the login.

03

A 9% price increase tested on new customers first de-risks the whole rollout.

04

Grandfather loyal accounts loudly. The goodwill is worth more than the delta.

05

Publish your pricing. Hiding it filters out the exact buyers you want most.

06

Review packaging quarterly. Pricing is a product surface, not a launch-day decision.
