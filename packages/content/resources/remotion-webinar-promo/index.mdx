## Webinar Promo

A cinematic 4-second promo clip designed to promote a live webinar. The composition opens on a deep slate background (`#1e293b`) with a layered teal glow radiating from center. A pulsing **FREE WEBINAR** badge springs into the top-left corner at frame 0, drawing the eye before the session title — "Mastering Modern React in 2026" — slides in from the left with a damped spring and a soft drop-shadow.

Starting at frame 25, the host block fades in below the title: a teal-bordered avatar circle sits beside the presenter's name and role in muted white typography. At frame 40 a date/time chip ("Tue Jun 18 / 3:00 PM EST") slides in from the right edge, completing the contextual information. A "Save Your Spot →" button then springs upward from scale 0, styled in solid teal with a shimmer sweep on loop. The final touch is an animated live viewer counter that ticks from 0 to 1,247 over 60 frames, reinforcing social proof before the composition fades out in the last 20 frames.

All configuration constants sit at the top of the file — swap `WEBINAR_TITLE`, `HOST_NAME`, `HOST_ROLE`, `DATE_TIME`, `BRAND_COLOR`, and `VIEWER_COUNT` to repurpose the template for any event in seconds.
