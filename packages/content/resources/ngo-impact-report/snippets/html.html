Program outcomes

Three flagship programs, the people behind them, and the results we promised to deliver.

Clean Water

Wells that never run dry

We completed 74 solar-pumped wells this year, cutting the average water-fetching walk from 4 hours to under 15 minutes and freeing girls to return to school.

74 of 80 wells goal · 92%
“Before, I missed class to carry water. Now the well is in our village — I have not missed a day of school in months.” — Amara, age 12, Mwanza district
Food & Nutrition

318,420 warm meals

Our network of 26 community kitchens served hot, nutritious meals through the lean season, while seed grants helped 1,140 families grow their own food.

318k of 410k meals goal · 78%
“The seed grant changed everything. This is the first year we grew enough to feed our children and still had a little to sell.” — Joseph & Ruth Okonkwo, smallholder farmers
Education

9,260 children, learning

Scholarships, school meals and twelve new classrooms kept 9,260 children enrolled — and 88% advanced to the next grade, our highest retention on record.

88% grade retention · 88%
“Our daughter is the first in our family to read. The teachers say she dreams of becoming a nurse.” — Fatima Bello, parent & volunteer
