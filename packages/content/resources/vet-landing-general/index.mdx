## General Vet Clinic Landing

A friendly, full marketing landing for a neighborhood veterinary clinic. It opens with a warm hero
and a prominent **Book now** call to action, then walks visitors through services, a meet-the-vets
preview, why-us care badges, testimonials, and a clear hours and location block.

The page is built around an approachable teal palette accented with warm coral, soft large radii and
gentle paw and heart motifs. A sticky header, smooth in-page navigation, a dismissible new-client
offer banner and reveal-on-scroll sections add polish without any libraries.

Everything is plain HTML, CSS custom properties and vanilla JavaScript. The booking buttons
demonstrate a lightweight inline confirmation flow you can wire to your real scheduler.
