## Highlights Recap Reel

A social-media-native highlights reel rendered entirely in Remotion at 1080×1920 (portrait 9:16). The animation opens with a glitch title — the word "HIGHLIGHTS" is rendered as three stacked divs offset horizontally (red, cyan, white channels) whose misalignment oscillates with the frame number, simulating an analog RGB split effect. A four-stop gradient progress bar ticks continuously along the top edge for the full five-second duration.

The body of the video is four clip panels, each occupying roughly one second of screen time inside its own `Sequence`. Every panel slides in from the right using a `spring()` transform, punches to scale on entry, and reveals location tag and bold caption text with a staggered `interpolate()` fade and upward slide. Each panel has a seeded-random dot texture, a diagonal stripe overlay, and a large watermark clip number — all deterministic so renders are frame-accurate. A white flash on panel entry mimics a hard cut transition.

The closing card fades in at frame 128 over a deep indigo-to-dark gradient background with a field of star dots. A gradient avatar circle springs in with `damping: 12` overshoot, followed by the username and a pill-shaped gradient CTA button. Twenty-eight confetti squares rain down with per-piece timing, drift, and rotation — all driven by `seededRand()` so no `Math.random()` is called at render time.
