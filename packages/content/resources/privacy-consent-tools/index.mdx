Before tracking users, you need consent. **Cookiebot** for automated cookie scanning and banners.
**Osano** for enterprise privacy programs with vendor monitoring. **Termly** for affordable
consent banners plus policy generation (also in [Legal Document Tools](/r/legal-document-tools)).
**Ketch** for API-first consent orchestration. Pair with [Product Analytics](/r/product-analytics)
to choose privacy-respecting tracking after consent.
