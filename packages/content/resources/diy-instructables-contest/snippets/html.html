Rules & judging

Read both columns before you submit. Entries that miss an eligibility line get bounced back with a note, not disqualified — you can fix and resubmit until the deadline.

Eligibility checklist

  • ✓
    Published during the window. The build log must go live between Mar 02 and the closing date shown above.
  • ✓
    Majority reclaimed wood. At least 70% of the visible material was offcut, pallet, salvage or otherwise second-hand.
  • ✓
    Your own work. Built by you, photographed by you. Collaborations are fine — name everyone.
  • ✓
    Five steps minimum. With at least one photo per step and a materials list with rough dimensions.
  • ✓
    Tagged correctly. Add the scrapwood26 tag before submitting so the sorter can find it.
  • ✓
    Safety shown honestly. Guards on, eyes covered. We won't feature a photo of an unguarded blade.

Judging criteria

Each entry is scored independently by three judges. Scores are weighted as follows.

  • Originality40%

    Did you make something we haven't seen ninety times this season?

  • Craftsmanship30%

    Joints, finish, and whether it will still be standing next winter.

  • Documentation20%

    Could a stranger rebuild it from your log alone?

  • Reuse10%

    How little new material you bought to get there.
