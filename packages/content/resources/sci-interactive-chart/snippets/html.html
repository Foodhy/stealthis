Temperature-dependence of phytoplankton net primary production across three abyssal monitoring stations

A. Rourke-Vance, J. Oduya, P. Lindqvist & the DEEPTRACE Consortium Institute for Pelagic Systems, Université de Tharsis · Preprint arXiv:2606.04417

Interactive Figure

Y scale

Stations

Three moorings spanning the polar front: S-12 Drake Passage, S-07 Weddell Gyre, and S-21 Kerguelen Plateau. Sampling depth integrated to the 1% light level.

Model

A modified Eppley curve was fit by least squares, P = P0·ek(T − T₀), with T₀ = 4 °C and station-specific k.

Reproducibility

All series here are seeded deterministically in the client so the figure renders identically offline. No external charting library is used — axes, gridlines and whiskers are inline SVG.
