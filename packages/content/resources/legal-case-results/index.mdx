## Case Results

A trust-first results page for a law firm. It opens with a navy **stat band** — total recovered for
clients, cases won, years in practice — framed by thin gold rules, then presents a filterable,
sortable list of case-result cards. Each card leads with the outcome amount or verdict, a practice-area
tag, a concise summary and the year the matter resolved.

Gold **filter chips** narrow the list by practice area client-side and announce the visible count to
assistive tech. A select control lets visitors **sort** by largest amount or most recent year. The
palette pairs navy and warm paper with a restrained gold accent, serif headings and Inter body text
for a classic, generous-whitespace feel.

Every interaction is vanilla JavaScript — no libraries, no build step. Filtering and sorting are
keyboard accessible, preserve focus styles and collapse gracefully on narrow screens.

> Illustrative UI only — placeholder results, not affiliated with any real law firm and not legal advice.
