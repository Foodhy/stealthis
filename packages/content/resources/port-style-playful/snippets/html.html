A little about me●

I'm Maya — a product designer who believes software should feel like a friend, not a form. I started out drawing comic strips, fell in love with the way a single curve can make a button feel kind, and never really stopped.

These days I lead design at a tiny studio, mentor at a code school, and fill far too many sketchbooks. When I'm not pushing pixels you'll find me baking questionable bread or doodling strangers on the metro.
