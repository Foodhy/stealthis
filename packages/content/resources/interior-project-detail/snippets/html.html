The Brief

A weekend farmhouse, softened for slow living.

The clients — a family of four splitting time between London and the country — asked us to strip back decades of dark panelling and reintroduce daylight without losing the building’s seventeenth-century bones. We reworked the ground-floor plan around a single, generous kitchen-and-hearth room, layering lime plaster, reclaimed oak and honest, tactile textiles.

Every surface was chosen to age gracefully: nothing here is meant to look new for long. The result is a home that feels quietly lived-in on the day the keys were handed back.
