## University Landing

A full marketing landing page for a fictional online university, Ashworth University, dressed in a scholarly palette of deep blue, crimson and ivory with serif headlines over a clean sans body. The hero pairs a prestige headline with an intake card that animates an SVG progress ring and lesson-style check-offs, while a sticky translucent nav gains a shadow on scroll and collapses into an accessible mobile menu.

Below the hero, an accreditation and statistics band runs count-up animations as it scrolls into view. The programs section offers a chip filter that narrows the degree and certificate grid by faculty (Business, Sciences, Arts, Technology) with an empty-state message, animated completion bars, level pills and difficulty badges. Faculty profiles, animated student-outcome bars, and a trio of alumni testimonials on a deep-blue panel build credibility before the closing admissions section.

The apply form validates name, email and program inline with accessible error messaging and aria states, resets on success, and surfaces a toast confirmation. Every section reveals on scroll, the layout reflows from a desktop grid down to a mobile-first single column at ~360px, and a reduced-motion media query disables animation for users who prefer it.

> Illustrative UI only — fictional courses, not a real learning platform.
