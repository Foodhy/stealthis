The studio

A name like a skeleton key.

Marrow is the core — the strategic truth inside a business. Plumb is the line — the craft that keeps everything straight and intentional. We named ourselves for the two halves of the work we do, because every brand we build starts with both.

We're a deliberately small studio. No pitch theatre, no fifty-slide decks of stock mood. Just a tight team that sits with your problem until the obvious-in-hindsight answer shows up — then draws it beautifully.

Work with us →
  • Truth over trend. We design what's true, not what's fashionable this quarter.
  • Systems, not logos. A mark is one part. We build the whole grammar.
  • Craft is the strategy. The way a thing is made is part of what it says.
