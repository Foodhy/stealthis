Pour-over 101

A great cup in four steps.

No barista certification required. This is the exact recipe we use at the roastery counter.

  1. 01

    Grind & weigh

    22g of beans, medium-fine, like coarse sand. Heat water to 96°C.

    ~1 min
  2. 02

    Bloom

    Pour 50g water, let the grounds breathe and degas for 30 seconds.

    0:30
  3. 03

    Pour in stages

    Add water in slow spirals up to 360g total, keeping the bed level.

    2:00
  4. 04

    Draw down & sip

    Drain by 3:30. Swirl, wait 60 seconds, and taste it cool slightly.

    3:30
