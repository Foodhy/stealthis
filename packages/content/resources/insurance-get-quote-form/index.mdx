## Get a Quote

A reassuring, trust-forward lead form for an insurance landing page. It opens with a confident
headline and a few proof points, then collects a coverage type, the basics needed to size a
premium, and contact details. Each field validates inline with clear error messages, and a privacy
note sets expectations before anyone hits submit.

On a valid submit the form swaps to a calm success state: a big, confident estimated **monthly
premium range** computed from the chosen coverage, age band and coverage amount, plus the next step
and a button to start over. All logic is vanilla JS — no libraries, no network calls.

The numbers are illustrative ranges only, designed to feel realistic without committing to a real
underwriting model.

> Illustrative UI only — **not** a real insurance quote or binding offer.
