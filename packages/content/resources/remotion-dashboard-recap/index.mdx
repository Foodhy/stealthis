## Dashboard Recap Video

A "Wrapped"-style monthly business recap rendered entirely in Remotion. The composition is divided into three timed segments that cross-fade with interpolated opacity transitions: a hero title slide, a horizontal bar chart of top-performing products, and a bold closing hero stat with confetti. All data is hardcoded as typed constants at the top of the file — swap them out to adapt the video to any month or brand.

Each segment uses a distinct animation vocabulary. The hero slide drives metric cards with `spring()` count-ups staggered 10 frames apart. The products chart grows each bar left-to-right using `spring()` width expansion, also staggered, while revenue labels count up in sync. The closing section uses a pulsing radial glow, a large gradient count-up number scaling in with a back-easing spring, and 36 deterministic confetti squares falling with per-piece delay, drift, and rotation driven entirely by `interpolate()` — no external randomness at render time.

Design choices include a deep `#0a0a0f` cinema background, a six-color palette (indigo, violet, cyan, emerald, amber, rose), linear-gradient filled bars with matching glow shadows, and a three-stop gradient text for the closing stat. A persistent radial vignette overlay unifies all three segments.

### Data format

All data lives in three typed constant arrays near the top of `react.tsx`:

- **`METRICS`** — three `{ label, value, prefix, suffix, color }` objects for the hero slide count-up cards (Revenue, Orders, New Users).
- **`PRODUCTS`** — five `{ name, revenue, color }` objects driving the horizontal bar chart. Bar widths are computed relative to `MAX_PRODUCT_REVENUE`, so simply add or remove entries and the chart rescales automatically.
- **`CONFETTI`** — generated deterministically from `seededRand()` (no `Math.random()`); change `CONFETTI_COLORS` or the array length to adjust density and palette.

To rebrand, update `COMPANY`, `MONTH`, and the `TARGET` closing stat value (currently `2_400_000`) at the top of the file.
