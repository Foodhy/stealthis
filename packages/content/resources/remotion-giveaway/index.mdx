## Giveaway Announcement

A punchy 4-second social-media giveaway announcement rendered entirely in Remotion. The composition opens with a "GIVEAWAY" headline exploding in via a spring with intentional overshoot — the rainbow gradient text (pink → yellow → cyan → purple) fills with a one-shot glint shimmer while 48 confetti particles of varying shapes drift across the canvas. Beneath the headline a gift-box illustration bobs gently and a typewriter effect spells out the prize line character-by-character, followed by a live entry counter that animates from zero up to 4,231 using an exponential easing curve.

The right column introduces three "how to enter" steps that stagger in from the left, each with a spring-popped emoji badge and an SVG checkmark. Colors alternate cyan → pink → yellow to give each step its own identity. A pulsing "Enter Now" button closes the composition: it scales in with a spring bounce, breathes with a subtle sinusoidal scale loop, emits a repeating ring pulse, and runs a continuous shimmer sweep until the global 20-frame fade-out ends the clip.

All configuration constants live at the top of the file — swap `PRIZE_LABEL`, `ENTRY_COUNT_TARGET`, `STEPS`, `CTA_LABEL`, and the three accent colors (`COLOR_PINK`, `COLOR_YELLOW`, `COLOR_CYAN`) to fit any brand or campaign in seconds.
