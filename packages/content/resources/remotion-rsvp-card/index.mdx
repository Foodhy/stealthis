## RSVP Confirmation Card

A clean, professional confirmation animation designed for social sharing and email embeds. The card enters with a satisfying spring scale-pop on a soft blue-green gradient background dotted with subtle grid points. A green circle grows in, then a white SVG checkmark draws itself in through stroke-dashoffset interpolation — the simplest trick that reads as "done" in every culture.

The event details are presented in a four-row list (event name, date/time, location, ticket info) each with a tinted green icon badge. Rows cascade in with staggered fade-and-slide transitions starting at frame 52, giving the viewer time to absorb each piece of information before the next arrives. A confetti burst at frame 40 adds a moment of delight without overwhelming the minimal aesthetic.

The final beat is a pair of action buttons — a primary green "Add to Calendar" and a ghost "View Details" — that spring up from below in the last 30 frames, closing the animation on a clear call to action.
