## Black Friday Deal

A dramatic 4-second promotional animation built entirely in Remotion. The pure-black background (#000000) creates maximum contrast for the neon yellow (#facc15) headline, which crashes in from above using a high-stiffness spring. The words "STARTS NOW" flash underneath with a rapid opacity pulse, adding urgency to the first second.

Three deal cards enter from alternating sides — left, right, left — staggered by 12 frames each. Each card shows a product name, the original crossed-out price, the sale price in bold yellow, and a circular percentage-off badge in the top-right corner. A radial glow behind the grid reinforces the premium feel. The final half-second fades everything out, keeping it clean for looping.

Customize `DEALS` to swap products, prices, and discount percentages. Adjust `ACCENT` to change the brand color, or tweak the spring `config` constants at the top for snappier or bouncier entrances.
