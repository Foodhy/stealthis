## Lyric Video Animation

A fully self-contained lyric video composition rendered at 1920×1080 and 30 fps. Five lyric segments — "Lost in the frequency", "Every beat finds me", "Resonating through the night", "Chasing soundwaves into light", and a symbolic closing glyph — each appear at a precise frame offset. Words enter with a spring-physics slide-up (translateY from +60 → 0, staggered 8 frames per word) and an opacity ramp, then exit with a smooth fade-and-lift as the next segment begins. The highlighted word in each phrase receives a `linear-gradient(135deg, #a855f7, #ec4899)` background-clip fill with a purple drop-shadow glow, making it pop against the deep near-black canvas without overwhelming the surrounding words.

The background is a dynamic gradient mesh: three large blurred color blobs (violet, indigo, and pink) drift independently via `Math.sin` oscillators, producing a slowly morphing depth effect similar to a CSS mesh gradient. Thirteen floating particle dots — colored purple, cyan, and pink — drift upward with per-particle speed, wobble amplitude, and phase, wrapping back to the bottom seamlessly. A 32-bar spectrum visualizer sits at the bottom of the frame at all times, its bar heights driven by four stacked sine waves at different frequencies and phases for an organic, non-repetitive audio-reactive look. During the brief instrumental interludes between lyric segments, six concentric rings pulse outward in expanding waves synchronized to a simulated beat frequency, with alternating purple and cyan borders and a glowing center orb.

Supporting layers include an SVG waveform underline beneath the lyric zone (path drawn from 80 interpolated sine points, stroked with a purple-to-cyan gradient and a Gaussian blur glow filter), corner bracket decorations that fade in at the opening, a progress bar footer showing track metadata ("frequency loop — deep pulses vol. 1") that advances from left to right across the full 240 frames, and global fade-in/fade-out envelopes at the very start and end of the composition for a professional broadcast finish.

> Simulated audio data — waveform values are generated mathematically. No real audio file is required.
