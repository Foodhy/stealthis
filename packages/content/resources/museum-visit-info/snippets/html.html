Getting Here

  • ▲
    By transit

    Metro Blue & Green lines to Cultural Mile, then a 3-minute walk north. Bus 12, 40, 88 stop at the door.

  • ■
    Parking

    Underground garage on Linden St., $6 flat with validation. 18 accessible bays on level P1.

  • ●
    By bike

    Sheltered racks at the West entrance; city bike-share dock across the plaza.
