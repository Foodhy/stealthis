Medication information

Amoxicillin 500 mg

Capsule, oral · Antibiotic (penicillin family)

Take 3 times a day 7-day course With or without food

Prescribed for Jordan Ellis by Dr. Lena Okafor · Northpoint Clinic

What it is for

Amoxicillin is an antibiotic used to treat bacterial infections such as chest, ear, throat and urinary infections. It works by stopping the growth of the bacteria causing your infection. It will not help with colds, flu or other infections caused by viruses.

How to take it

  1. 1

    One capsule, three times a day

    Roughly every 8 hours — for example morning, afternoon and night.

  2. 2

    Swallow whole with a glass of water

    Do not crush or chew. You can take it with or without food.

  3. 3

    Finish the full 7-day course

    Keep going even if you feel better, so the infection clears completely.

Common side effects

These are usually mild and often settle on their own. They affect roughly 1 in 100 people.

  • • Feeling sick (nausea)
  • • Loose or more frequent stools
  • • Mild stomach discomfort
  • • A harmless change in taste

Taking each dose with a little food can help if your stomach feels unsettled.

!

Important — get help straight away if…

  • You get a skin rash, itching, swelling of the face, lips or throat.
  • You have trouble breathing or feel faint after a dose.
  • You develop severe or watery diarrhoea that does not stop.

These can be signs of a serious allergic reaction. Call emergency services or go to your nearest emergency department.

Do & Don’t

Do

  • ✓ Take it at evenly spaced times
  • ✓ Drink plenty of water
  • ✓ Store below 25°C, away from light
  • ✓ Tell us about other medicines

Don’t

  • ✕ Drink alcohol heavily
  • ✕ Share with anyone else
  • ✕ Double up on a missed dose
  • ✕ Stop early without asking us
⏱

Missed a dose?

Take it as soon as you remember. If your next dose is due within 2 hours, skip the missed one and carry on as normal — never take a double dose to make up for it.
