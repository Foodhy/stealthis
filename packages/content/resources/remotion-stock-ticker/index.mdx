## Stock Ticker Reel

A six-second Remotion composition that evokes a financial terminal at market close. The top half is dominated by three large market index cards — **SX500**, **TECH-X**, and **GLOBAL** — each staggered into view with a scale spring, followed by a monospace value that counts up from zero and a colored badge (▲ green / ▼ red) that slides in once the number settles. The layout is deliberately spacious so the numbers breathe.

Below the indices, two featured stock cards animate upward from a gentle spring offset. Each card contains the ticker symbol, full company name, a live-counting price, a delta pill, and a 52-point SVG sparkline that draws itself from left to right using a progress interpolation. The sparkline includes a translucent fill area and a glowing endpoint dot. At the very bottom of the frame, a full-width ticker bar fades in at frame 45 and scrolls ten fictional symbols continuously at a constant pixel speed, looping seamlessly via a tripled array.

Design-wise the composition leans into a dark terminal aesthetic (`#080b10` background, monospace fonts for numeric data, dot-grid texture at low opacity) counterbalanced by vibrant per-symbol accent colors — indigo, cyan, amber, violet, sky, orange — and soft radial glows to add depth. All data is entirely fictional.

### Data format

All data is hardcoded as constants at the top of `react.tsx`. The `INDICES` array holds three `{ symbol, value, delta }` objects for the large count-up cards. `FEATURED` holds two `{ symbol, name, price, delta, color, sparkline }` objects — `sparkline` is an array of 18 normalized `0–1` values that the `Sparkline` component maps to SVG polyline coordinates. `TICKER_STOCKS` is an array of ten `{ symbol, price, delta, color }` entries that scroll across the bottom bar. To customize: swap in your own symbols, adjust the `sparkline` arrays to reflect different trend shapes, change `color` values per stock, or update the title string in `TitleHeader`.
