Overview

Why Isla Verde

Isla Verde rises straight out of the Coral Sea in folds of terraced green. Its capital, Porto Lima, wraps a horseshoe harbour in pastel houses; beyond it, switchback roads climb past coffee farms into a cloud forest that keeps the whole island impossibly lush.

Days here run on island time — long market mornings, a two-hour lunch, and a sunset everyone stops to watch. This guide walks you through the highlights, the cooking you cannot miss, and where to lay your head.

    Know before you go

    Local tips

    🌦️

    Pack for two climates

    Coast is hot and humid; the cloud forest is 10°C cooler and wet. A light shell earns its place.

    ⛴️

    Ferries beat roads

    The coastal ferry loop is faster and prettier than the switchback road. Buy a 3-day hopper pass.

    💵

    Carry small cash

    Markets and ferries are cash-first. ATMs cluster in Porto Lima, so stock up before heading north.

    🌅

    Sunset is sacred

    Book dinner for after dusk — locals down tools at golden hour, and so should you.
