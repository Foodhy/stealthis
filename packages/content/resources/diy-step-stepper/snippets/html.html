Replace the Cooling Fan

Voltcraft Nomad 14 laptop · Fan module FAN-VN14-B · Torque all screws to snug, not stripped.

0/6 steps done
0%
  1. STEP 01 POWER OFF ~2 min

    Power down & disconnect

    Shut the laptop down fully (no sleep mode), unplug the charger, and hold the power button for 10 seconds to drain residual charge.

  2. STEP 02 ~5 min

    Remove the bottom cover

    Undo the 9 × T5 Torx screws around the base (the two under the rear feet are longer — keep them separate), then pry from the hinge side with a plastic spudger.

  3. STEP 03 FRAGILE ~3 min

    Disconnect battery & fan cable

    Lift the battery connector straight up with a fingernail, then flip the tiny latch on the fan's 4-pin JST connector and slide the ribbon free. Never pull by the wires.

  4. STEP 04 ~8 min

    Lift out the old fan

    Remove the 3 × PH00 screws holding the fan shroud, peel back the strip of foil tape over the heat-pipe joint, and lift the fan out at an angle. Note the airflow arrow on the housing.

  5. STEP 05 FAN-VN14-B ~10 min

    Seat the new fan

    Drop the replacement fan into the bay, airflow arrow pointing at the rear vent. Re-fit the 3 shroud screws in a triangle pattern, re-lay the foil tape, and click the fan cable back in until the latch snaps.

  6. STEP 06 FINAL CHECK ~5 min

    Close up & spin test

    Reconnect the battery, snap the cover on, and refit all 9 screws (long ones at the rear). Boot up and run a fan test — a healthy idle spin is ≈ 2,400 RPM with no ticking.
