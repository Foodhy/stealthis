**PostHog** is the default for startups that want analytics, replay, flags, and experiments in one place.
**Plausible** and **Umami** for privacy-friendly, lightweight web stats. **Amplitude** and **Mixpanel** for
deep behavioral analysis. **Google Analytics 4** when you need free analytics with Google Ads. **June** for
auto-generated B2B SaaS reports with minimal setup.
