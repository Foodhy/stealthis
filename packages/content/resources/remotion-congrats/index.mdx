## Congratulations Animation

A vibrant, fully animated congratulations card rendered with Remotion at 30 fps. The animation opens with a burst of 60 deterministic confetti pieces — squares, circles, and rectangles — launching from the center using seeded-random trajectories so each render is identical. Twelve radial burst lines expand outward on the first beat, followed by a pulsing conic-gradient glow ring that bathes the composition in rainbow light.

The star of the show is the `CONGRATULATIONS!` headline, where each of the sixteen characters springs in individually using staggered spring animations with damping 10 and stiffness 200 — producing the satisfying overshoot bounce that signals celebration. Each letter cycles through a different saturated color (yellow, orange, pink, purple, blue, green, red, cyan), and the exclamation mark gets a slightly larger scale for emphasis. A floating SVG trophy icon appears early, gently bobbing with a sine-wave float to keep the scene alive throughout the four seconds.

The composition closes with a gradient name badge — editable via the `PERSON_NAME` constant — that slides in from below alongside a supporting subtitle line. Twelve SVG sparkle stars scatter around the text zone at staggered offsets, each popping in with its own spring entry and continuously twinkling. The background uses layered radial pastel gradients to stay bright and airy, letting the colorful confetti and text read clearly against the white ground.
