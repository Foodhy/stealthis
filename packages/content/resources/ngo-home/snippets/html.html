Our approach

What we do

Three things, done patiently and well — so the water keeps coming.

⛏️

We build

Boreholes, hand pumps, and gravity-fed systems engineered for the terrain and the community that will use them.

412 wells · 38 districts
🧰

We train

Local water committees and paid technicians who can fix a pump in an afternoon instead of waiting on a city.

1,240 technicians certified
📡

We monitor

Sensors and field visits track every site, so a broken pump is a phone alert — not a year of empty buckets.

94% uptime, lifetime
