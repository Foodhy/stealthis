For referring veterinarians

A referral process built around your practice

Send the case, keep the relationship. Your client returns to you with a full report and a clear follow-up plan.

  1. 1

    Submit the referral

    Use the form, fax records to (555) 204-7790, or call our coordinator directly.

  2. 2

    We triage within 2 hours

    A specialist reviews history and contacts your client to schedule, urgent cases same day.

  3. 3

    Co-managed treatment

    You receive imaging, labs and a written plan, with phone access to the specialist.

  4. 4

    Patient returns to you

    We hand care back with discharge notes so continuity stays in your practice.
