How it works

From text to talking in three calls.

01

Stream text in

Send tokens as they generate. Vox starts speaking before your sentence is finished.

vox.speak({ voice: "aria",
  text: stream })
02

Shape the emotion

Nudge tone, speed, and stability with simple controls — or let the model infer mood from context.

style: { emotion: "calm",
  pace: 0.9 }
03

Get audio out

Receive a low-latency PCM stream you can pipe straight to the browser, phone, or your DAW.

audio.play(vox.stream)
// 120ms to first byte
