01 / Core Pillar

BLADE-DANCE
COMBAT

Chain stances mid-swing. Every weapon in Hollow Reign carries three stances — Tempest, Bulwark, and Veil — and switching between them at the apex of a combo triggers a stance echo that rewrites your next three attacks.

No cooldown bars. No ability bloat. Just timing, spacing, and a stagger system that punishes button mashing and rewards reads.

  • 3 stances per weapon, swappable mid-combo
  • Directional parries with posture-break finishers
  • Boss duels tuned for zero-damage mastery runs

02 / Core Pillar

A KINGDOM
WITHOUT LOADING WALLS

Vel Adris is one continuous map: four biomes stitched together by grapple lines, ash storms, and collapsed leyways. If you can see a spire on the horizon, you can climb it — and something is buried under it.

The Cartographer's Eye marks anomalies, not objectives. The map fills with what you discover, never with what a quest log demands.

  • 4 hand-built biomes, zero loading screens
  • Grapple, glide, and wall-run traversal kit
  • 200+ optional vaults, shrines, and world bosses

03 / Core Pillar

FORGE WEAPONS
THAT REMEMBER

Fuse any two weapon cores at a Nullforge bench to create a hybrid that inherits a moveset from one parent and an elemental memory from the other. Kill a flame revenant with it, and the blade keeps a spark of that fight forever.

Weapons level with deeds, not grind: each named kill etches a rune that permanently alters the weapon's stance echoes.

  • 60+ base weapons, thousands of fusion outcomes
  • Deed-runes earned from named enemies
  • No durability, no dismantle regret — refund any fusion

04 / Core Pillar

FOUR CROWNS,
ONE CAMPAIGN

The entire campaign — every cutscene, every vault, every ending — supports drop-in co-op for up to four players. Progression is shared both ways: whatever your warband unlocks, you keep in your own world.

Warband revives cost banner charges, not respawns, and the final battle scales its phases to your party — solo crowns are a different fight than four-player ones.

  • Full campaign drop-in / drop-out, 1–4 players
  • Cross-platform warbands with shared stash
  • Bidirectional progression — host nothing, lose nothing
