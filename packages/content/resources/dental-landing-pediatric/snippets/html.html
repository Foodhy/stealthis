The first visit

Three easy steps, zero nerves

We keep it short, gentle, and fun. Here's exactly how your child's first appointment goes.

  1. 1

    Meet & explore

    A friendly hello, a tour of the room, and a ride in the "moon chair." Your child touches every tool before it's used.

    ~5 min
  2. 2

    Count the teeth

    A gentle, tickle-free checkup and cleaning with our sugar-bug detector. We narrate every step so nothing feels surprising.

    ~15 min
  3. 3

    Prizes & plan

    A sticker, a new toothbrush, and a simple at-home plan for you. High-fives all around before you go.

    ~5 min
