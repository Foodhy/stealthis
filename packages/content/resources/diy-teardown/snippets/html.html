      Step-by-step teardown

      Four chapters, bench-tested twice. Total time on the clock: 22 minutes.

      1. STEP 01

        Peel the fabric grille

        The grille sleeve is tensioned, not glued. Work a plastic pick under the bottom hem and roll it upward like a sock. The LED diffuser ring underneath lifts off four rubber posts.

        ⛏ PLASTIC PICK ⏱ 2 MIN
      2. STEP 02

        Free the driver assembly

        Four T6 Torx screws hold the 40 mm driver basket. The voice-coil leads terminate in a 2-pin JST-SH — pull the connector, never the wires. The mic array flex rides on the same bracket.

        🔧 TORX T6 🔩 TWEEZERS ⏱ 5 MIN
        CAUTION The mic flex cable is 0.3 mm thick and folded twice. Creasing it kills far-field pickup.
      3. STEP 03

        Lift the main board

        The PCB sits on three brass standoffs with a single T6 at the antenna corner. Disconnect the battery FIRST (2-pin JST-PH), then the halo LED flex (12-pin ZIF — flip the black latch up).

        🔧 TORX T6 ⚡ ESD STRAP ⏱ 6 MIN
        HAZARD Board is live until the battery lead is pulled. Shorting the exposed test pads against the shielding can cook the PMIC.
      4. STEP 04

        Extract the battery

        The 1.4 Ah pouch cell is held by two stretch-release adhesive tabs. Pull each tab slowly at a shallow angle — around 15 cm of stretch each. If a tab snaps, switch to isopropyl and a card.

        💧 ISOPROPYL 90% 🃏 PLASTIC CARD ⏱ 9 MIN
        HAZARD Never pry a pouch cell with metal. A punctured cell vents hot electrolyte. Work over a fire-safe mat with the cell below 30% charge.

      04

      Verdict

      Where the Puck Mini earns its 7 — and where it drops points.

      ＋ What we liked

      • Standard Torx T6 fasteners throughout — zero proprietary screws.
      • Modular driver, board and battery separate in under ten minutes.
      • Stretch-release battery tabs instead of hard glue.
      • Every connector is keyed; wrong reassembly is nearly impossible.
      • Silkscreened test pads are labelled on the board itself.

      － What hurts repair

      • Fabric grille stretches out after 2–3 removals; no spare sold.
      • Battery is a custom pouch — no listed replacement part yet.
      • Mic flex is fragile and routed under the driver bracket.
      • LED diffuser posts shear off if the grille is pulled straight up.
      7/10 REPAIRABLE
