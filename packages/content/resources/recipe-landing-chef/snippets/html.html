Chef Hélène Verel
Philosophy

We cook the calendar, not the cookbook.

Hélène Verel trained in Lyon and Copenhagen before returning to open Atelier Verel with no fixed menu — only a relationship with twenty growers, two fishermen and a forager who arrives unannounced.

Each evening begins where the market ends. What you eat tonight could not have existed last week, and will not exist next.

  • 🌿 Sourced within a single day’s drive
  • 🔥 Cooked over wood and embers
  • 🍷 Paired by our cellar, glass by glass

— Hélène Verel
