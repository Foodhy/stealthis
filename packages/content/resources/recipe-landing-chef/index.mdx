## Restaurant Chef / Fine Dining landing

A refined single-page landing for **Atelier Verel**, a fictional twelve-seat chef's
counter. The mood is couture: a charcoal gradient hero with gold hairlines, a Fraunces
serif headline, and a Michelin-style overline, all set against ivory body type. Soft
radial "food photography" blobs drift behind the hero as a parallax hint, and every
section reveals on scroll with a gentle stagger.

The body walks a guest down the experience — a signature-dishes grid of gradient plates,
a chef philosophy strip with drop-cap prose and a portrait card, a nine-course tasting
menu teaser with per-guest pricing, glowing press quotes, and a reservation panel. Each
dish opens a focus-trapped lightbox with its course, tasting note, and a reserve CTA;
the reservation form validates name, email, date, and party size, then confirms with a
toast and an `aria-live` status message.

Everything is vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no libraries: food imagery is pure
CSS gradients plus emoji, the layout collapses cleanly to a single column on mobile, and
all controls are keyboard-usable with visible focus and reduced-motion support.

> Illustrative UI only — recipes & nutrition data are fictional, not dietary advice.
