## Vaccination Tracker

A warm, approachable health panel for a single pet. The header introduces the patient — a beagle
named **Biscuit** — alongside an animated SVG progress ring that reports overall vaccination
coverage. Below it, vaccines are grouped into **Core** and **Non-core** sections, each row carrying
a status badge (_Up to date_, _Due soon_ or _Overdue_), the last-given date and the next-due date.

Overdue and due-soon rows surface a teal **Schedule** button. Clicking it confirms inline, marks the
vaccine up to date, advances its next-due date, and recalculates the coverage ring and the summary
counts — all in vanilla JS with no libraries. Status changes are announced via an `aria-live`
region for screen-reader users.

The layout uses soft teal and coral accents, large rounded cards and a paw motif, collapsing to a
single comfortable column on narrow screens.

> Illustrative UI only — **not** for real veterinary use.
