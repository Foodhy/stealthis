## Coupon Reveal Animation

A warm, playful 3-second coupon reveal built entirely in Remotion. The deep stone background (#1c1917) sets a rich canvas for the cream coupon card (#fef3c7) that scales up from zero via a bouncy spring in the first half-second. The top edge of the card features a dashed amber perforation line, and an SVG scissors icon tracks smoothly across it — cutting the coupon free with an interpolated X-position tween.

The discount code "SAVE20" types out one character at a time using a frame-based slice, complete with a blinking cursor. A massive "20% OFF" headline bounces in with a high-stiffness spring overshoot, drawing the eye to the core value proposition. Below the code, small confetti dots burst radially from the card center with spring-based scale and rotation, adding celebration energy. Finally, "Valid until Jun 30" fades in softly at the bottom with a gentle upward drift.

Customize `COUPON_CODE`, `DISCOUNT_TEXT`, `EXPIRY_TEXT`, and the accent color `AMBER` at the top of the file. Adjust `CARD_SPRING` damping for a bouncier or more settled card entrance. The `CONFETTI_DOTS` array holds position/color overrides for the particle burst.
