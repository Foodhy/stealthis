Abstract

Sparse mixtures of experts (MoE) scale model capacity by routing each token to a small subset of specialized sub-networks. We show that under covariate shift the learned gating distribution exhibits a measurable spectral drift: the leading singular values of the router weight matrix migrate toward a degenerate regime, collapsing effective expert diversity from 7.9 to 2.3 active experts per batch. We introduce SpecReg, a lightweight spectral regularizer that penalizes singular-value concentration during fine-tuning. Across three shifted benchmarks SpecReg recovers +4.6 points of out-of-distribution accuracy at a 1.2% compute overhead, with no degradation on the in-distribution split.
