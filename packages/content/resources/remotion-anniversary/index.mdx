## Anniversary Video

A warm, cinematic anniversary card built entirely in Remotion with zero external dependencies. The background is a deep amber-to-black radial gradient layered with three large soft glow orbs that subtly pulse using Math.sin, setting an intimate mood from the first frame. Fifteen floating dot particles oscillate independently on both axes using sine math — each with its own speed multiplier and phase offset — giving the scene a living, breathing quality throughout.

The animation unfolds in clear acts: the headline "Happy Anniversary" sweeps in from the left on a spring with a serif font and warm glow, followed by a year badge inside a glowing circle ring that bounces in with a stiff spring (damping 11, stiffness 180) and continues to pulse gently. Two name placeholders slide in from opposite sides flanking an SVG heart shape that scales up on its own spring, then pulses rhythmically once the timeline reaches its final phase. The Past/Present/Future timeline bar at the bottom uses a growing connector line driven by spring, with each dot and label fading in sequentially. The finale spawns six small hearts orbiting outward before the entire scene fades to black.

All text, color, and name values are declared as constants at the top of the file — swap `NAME_LEFT`, `NAME_RIGHT`, and `YEARS` to personalize the card without touching any animation logic.
