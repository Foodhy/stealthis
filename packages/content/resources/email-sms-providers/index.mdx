For transactional email in a modern stack, **Resend** is the default pick — simple API and great React Email
integration. **Postmark** wins when deliverability is non-negotiable. **SendGrid** handles massive volume.
**AWS SES** is the cost-effective choice on AWS. **Loops** and **Customer.io** add lifecycle and behavioral
messaging — see [Email Marketing Tools](/r/email-marketing-tools) for campaigns. Need SMS or voice?
**Twilio** is the one-stop communications layer.
