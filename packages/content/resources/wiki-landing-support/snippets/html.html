Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

Go to the login page and choose Forgot password. We'll email a secure reset link to your account address — it expires after 30 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check spam or use the Resend button. SSO users reset their password with their identity provider instead.

Open Settings → Billing → Invoices. Every invoice is available as a PDF, with your tax ID and billing address included. Workspace owners and billing admins can download invoices; other roles can request access from an owner.

In the Aurora dashboard, go to Developers → API keys → New key. Give it a name, choose a scope, and copy the secret immediately — it is shown only once. Use it as a bearer token:

curl https://api.nimbus.dev/v1/projects \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer nbs_live_••••••••"

Rotate keys regularly and never commit them to source control.

Your workspace stays active until the end of the current billing period, then moves to the free Starter tier. No data is deleted — paid features are simply paused. You can re-subscribe at any time and pick up exactly where you left off.
