Need 3D assets from a prompt or a photo? Self-host **Hunyuan3D** for open, high-quality
meshes (there's a live demo), run **Modly** locally on your GPU, or use **Meshy** / **Tripo**
in the cloud for fast game-ready assets. Compare by input, where it runs, and openness.
