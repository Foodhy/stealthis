From our kitchen

We cook the way we were taught — slowly, and with the windows open.

Three generations have stirred pots at this same scrubbed table. The recipes here aren't fussy; they're the kind you learn by smell and repetition — a pinch more salt, another splash of the good oil, a loaf left to rise overnight while the house sleeps.

Every dish in the book has been cooked on a weeknight, fed to neighbours, and earned its splatter. We hope they earn a few of yours, too.

— Mara & the Copper Kettle table
