Picking an engine? Godot is the open-source all-rounder, Unreal leads on high-end 3D, Unity
is the cross-platform/mobile workhorse, and GameMaker is the fastest on-ramp for 2D. Prefer
no-code? Construct 3. Web-first? Phaser (JS 2D) or PlayCanvas (3D). Code-first/open source?
Bevy (Rust), Defold and Cocos (mobile 2D), or LÖVE (Lua). Each card has a beginner video —
compare by license, language, and what you're building.
