Our Story

Three generations of the right way.

Vincent Moretti opened the shop with one chair and a borrowed mirror in 1962. Six decades on, his grandson still strops the razors at close and the same brass register sits by the door.

We never chased the trends. We kept the towels hot, the talk honest, and the prices fair — and the line out the door took care of itself.
