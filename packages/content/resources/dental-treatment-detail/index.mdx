A single-treatment detail page for a fictional clear-aligner service at Brightwater Dental. The layout leads with a reassuring hero — treatment name, a short benefits summary, trust badges, and a soft gradient smile illustration — followed by a plain-language "what it is" card and a highlight strip of key facts (duration, visits, comfort).

The core interaction is a five-step treatment timeline: clicking any step expands its detail panel and animates a progress rail, so visitors can walk through scan, plan, aligners, check-ins, and retainer at their own pace. A cost-range estimator lets users pick case complexity and number of arches; the displayed price band, monthly financing figure, and expected duration recalculate live with a subtle count-up. The FAQ is a keyboard-accessible accordion, and a floating "Book a consultation" button opens a confirmation toast.

Everything is vanilla HTML, CSS, and JS with a clinical light theme — lots of white, blue and mint accents, rounded cards, and gentle hover and focus states tuned for WCAG AA contrast and full keyboard use.

> Illustrative UI only — **not** intended for real medical use.
