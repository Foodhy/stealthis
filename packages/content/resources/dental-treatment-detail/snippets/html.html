What it is

Straighten your teeth, discreetly

Clear aligners are a series of custom, BPA-free trays worn over your teeth. Each set is worn for one to two weeks, moving your teeth a fraction of a millimetre at a time toward the final position your dentist plans in advance. Because they're removable, you eat, brush, and floss exactly as you do today.

The process

Five simple steps

Select a step to see what happens and what you'll need to do.

  1. A quick 3D intraoral scan replaces goopy impressions. Your dentist reviews your bite, discusses goals, and answers questions. No commitment on day one.

  2. We map every tooth movement digitally and share an animated preview of your projected smile, the number of trays, and the expected timeline before you decide.

  3. Wear each set 20–22 hours a day, swapping to the next tray every 1–2 weeks. Remove them only to eat and clean your teeth. Most people adjust within a day or two.

  4. Brief visits every 6–8 weeks let your dentist confirm progress and hand over your next sets of trays. Remote monitoring is available between visits.

  5. Once you reach your goal, a custom retainer keeps your new smile in place. We recommend nightly wear to protect your investment for years to come.
