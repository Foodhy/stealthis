## Booking Search

The entry point of a hotel's direct-booking funnel. A full-bleed hero hosts the search card: pick a destination/property, choose check-in and check-out dates (with validation so check-out is always after check-in), and open the **guests & rooms** popover to step adults, children and rooms up or down. A live summary line shows the number of nights and the party, and the search button assembles a query summary. Below the hero sit a "why book direct" perks strip and a few featured stays.
