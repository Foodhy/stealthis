Good to know

How do revisions work?

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds on the concept and layout. Extra rounds are billed at a flat $180 each, agreed before we begin.

Do you supply the furniture?

We provide a full shopping list with trade pricing where available. Procurement and installation are included from the Signature tier upward.

What if my project grows?

Add rooms with the stepper above and the estimate updates instantly. You can also upgrade tiers mid-project — we credit what you've already paid.

Is the timeline guaranteed?

Timelines start once we receive your brief and deposit. Delivery windows are per-room and shown on each card; we build in a two-week buffer for trade lead times.
