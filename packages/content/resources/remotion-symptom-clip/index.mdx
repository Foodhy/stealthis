## Symptom Info Clip

A clean, educational 7-second vertical short built entirely in Remotion, designed to be shown on waiting-room screens or embedded in patient-portal apps. The composition opens on a deep `#0a1a18` background with a subtle radial teal glow at the top, immediately followed by the headline "🤧 Common Cold — Know the Signs" dropping in from above the frame on a crisp spring. A horizontal rule fades in beneath it to visually separate the header from the symptom grid below.

Four symptom chips — Runny Nose, Sore Throat, Mild Fever, and Body Aches — animate in two per row with a 15-frame stagger between each chip. Every chip is a rounded card carrying a left-side emoji icon, a white label, and a small colored urgency dot (warn amber or coral) in the top-right corner, all sitting on a slightly elevated surface created with a semi-transparent teal background and a subtle inset box shadow. After the grid is fully visible, a "When to See a Doctor" panel slides up from below the fold: it has a bold left border in the danger red color and lists three critical warning signs that warrant an immediate clinic visit. The composition ends with a muted footer bearing the clinic name and a brief call to action.

Every color, string, and timing constant is isolated at the top of the file. To repurpose the clip for a different illness, update `TITLE`, `SYMPTOM_CHIPS`, and `DANGER_SIGNS`. To change branding, update `CLINIC_NAME` and `FOOTER_CTA`. The stagger delay is controlled by `CHIP_STAGGER_FRAMES`.

### Customization

- `TITLE` — headline text (emoji + condition name)
- `CLINIC_NAME` / `FOOTER_CTA` — bottom branding line
- `SYMPTOM_CHIPS` — array of `{ emoji, label, dot }` objects; `dot` accepts `"warn"` or `"coral"`
- `DANGER_SIGNS` — array of strings for the when-to-see-a-doctor panel
- `CHIP_STAGGER_FRAMES` — frames between each chip entrance (default `15`)
- `SPRING_CONFIG` — shared spring damping / stiffness across all motion
- Color constants at the top — swap the entire palette without touching layout logic
