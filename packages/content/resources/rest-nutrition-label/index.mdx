## Dish Nutrition Label

Nutrition panel in the restaurant's warm aesthetic (not the clinical government style). Shows: calories large, macro bars (protein/carbs/fat as %-of-serving colored bar), full nutrient table (sodium, fiber, sugar, cholesterol, vitamins), allergen badge row, and a serving-size stepper (×1 to ×4) that scales all displayed values proportionally. Dish selector dropdown at top switches between 4 dishes.
