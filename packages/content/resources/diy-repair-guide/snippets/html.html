STEP 1

Power down & discharge

  • Hold the power button on the right cup for 5 seconds until the status LED goes dark.
  • Play audio at low volume for a few minutes beforehand if the battery is above 50% — a partially drained cell is safer to handle.
  • Unplug any USB-C or 3.5 mm cables.
⚠ BATTERY SAFETY

Never work on a charging device. Lithium-polymer cells can vent or ignite if punctured while energised.

STEP 2

Remove the right ear cushion

  • Grip the cushion at the 3 and 9 o'clock positions and rotate it counter-clockwise about 15° until the bayonet tabs release.
  • Lift the cushion straight off. No tools needed — it should release with light force.
  • Set it fabric-side up so the memory foam keeps its shape.
ℹ PRO TIP

Photograph the cushion orientation before removal — the oval pad only reseats correctly one way.

STEP 3

Remove the driver plate screws

  • 3× P00 4.2 MM around the plate rim — orange dots in the figure.
  • 1× T5 6.0 MM at the hinge side — green dot. This one is longer; keep it separate.
  • Park screws on a magnetic mat in the same layout as the cup so nothing gets swapped.
⚠ CAUTION

The P00 heads strip easily. Press down firmly and turn slowly — if a screw resists, re-seat the driver rather than forcing it.

STEP 4

Lift the driver plate

  • Slide an opening pick into the seam near the USB-C port and work around the rim to free the friction clips — four soft clicks.
  • Hinge the plate open like a book toward the headband. A short ribbon cable still ties it to the driver.
  • Rest the plate against the yoke; do not let it dangle by the ribbon.
⚠ PRY GENTLY

Insert the pick no deeper than 3 mm — the driver surround sits just inside the rim and tears easily. Never pry with metal.

STEP 5

Disconnect the battery

  • Locate connector J4 · BTB 4-PIN on the edge of the logic board, marked red in the figure.
  • Slip the flat end of a nylon spudger under the connector and lever it straight up — it pops off like a snap button.
  • With the battery isolated, the rest of the repair is de-energised.
⚠ DANGER — SHORT RISK

Do not touch the board with metal tools while the battery is connected. Bridging the J4 pads can destroy the charge controller.

STEP 6

Remove the old cell

  • Find the white stretch-release pull tab at the bottom edge of the cell.
  • Grip it with tweezers and pull slowly and parallel to the cup — the strip stretches to ~10× its length before releasing.
  • If the tab snaps, wick a drop of isopropyl alcohol under the cell, wait 60 seconds, and lift with a plastic pick from the tab edge.
⚠ DANGER — PUNCTURE

Never bend, crease, or lever against the pouch cell. If the old battery is swollen, stop and take the unit to a battery recycler.

STEP 7

Seat the new cell

  • Clean the recess with isopropyl alcohol and let it flash off.
  • Lay the two new strips from kit VX2-ADH-KIT inside the dashed outline, then peel the top liners.
  • Orient the new VX2-CELL-620 with its lead toward J4, lower it in, and press for 10 seconds.
ℹ CHECK FIRST

Dry-fit the cell before peeling the liners — the adhesive grabs on first contact and repositioning weakens it.

STEP 8

Reconnect & test

  • Align the J4 · BTB 4-PIN connector over its socket and press with a fingertip until it clicks flat.
  • Before closing anything, hold the power button — the LED should breathe green.
  • Power back off, then hinge the driver plate closed until every clip clicks.
⚠ TEST BEFORE CLOSING

If the LED blinks amber twice, the connector is half-seated. Disconnect fully and press it home again — never force a misaligned BTB.

Almost there

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order — screws in step 3 go back last, and the green 1× T5 6.0 MM returns to the hinge side only.
