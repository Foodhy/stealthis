## Onboarding Welcome Clip

An onboarding welcome composition: confetti burst of colored dots radiates from center (using multiple small divs interpolated outward), "Welcome aboard! 🎉" title springs in bold, avatar initials circle bounces in, and three quick-start action cards appear with staggered spring entrances (Connect account, Set up workspace, Invite team).
