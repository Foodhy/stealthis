A polished marketing landing page for **Ambit**, a fictional members-only dating community aimed at busy professionals. The design leans on a vetted, high-trust angle: every headline, badge, and card reinforces that members are ID-verified and hand-reviewed. A coral-to-violet gradient hero pairs an aspirational headline with a compact **apply-to-join** form, while a live ticker counts applications reviewed this week to build gentle social proof.

Below the fold, a three-step vetting explainer walks visitors through *Apply → Verify → Match*, followed by curated match-preview cards showing fictional professionals with profession tags and mutual-interest chips. A rotating testimonial carousel highlights couples who met on the platform, and a membership-tier section contrasts a free and premium plan with a toggle for monthly versus annual pricing.

Interactions are all vanilla JS: the application form validates inline and fires a toast on submit, the testimonial carousel auto-advances with manual controls and dots, the FAQ uses an accessible accordion, the pricing toggle swaps prices with a subtle count-up, and the live application ticker increments on an interval. Everything is keyboard-usable with visible focus rings and collapses cleanly to a single column on narrow screens.
