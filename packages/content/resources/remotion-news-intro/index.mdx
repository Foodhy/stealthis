## News Show Intro

A production-ready TV news show opening sequence built entirely with Remotion and inline React styles — no external assets, no images, no dependencies beyond `remotion` itself. The composition opens on a deep dark background (`#0a0e1a`) with an animated SVG grid overlaying horizontal and vertical lines that fade in staggered across the first 40 frames, accompanied by a globe wireframe constructed from concentric elliptical latitude arcs and longitude sweep paths, each driven by `interpolate` on `strokeDashoffset` for a clean draw-on effect.

Scene 2 delivers the hero moment: the `NNX` network logo springs in from scale 0.2 using a spring with `damping: 18, stiffness: 120`, surrounded by a deep red glow shadow (`box-shadow` with three layered rgba values). Below it, each letter of `NIGHTLY NEWS` enters individually with a 3-frame stagger, sliding from `-20px` with an `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` curve and fading to full opacity. Scene 3 plots three world coverage cities — Paris, Tokyo, and New York — as colored dots at percentage-based positions over the composition canvas. Each location springs in with `damping: 12, stiffness: 200` and emits a repeating pulse ring that expands and fades every 45 frames. Dashed connector lines join the dots at frame 141.

The final scene (frames 160–210) assembles the complete title card: the network badge on a solid red block, `NIGHTLY NEWS` in 72px bold white with 8px letter-spacing, a gold separator line flanking the date string, and the network tagline fading in last in muted white. A persistent bottom chyron bar slides up from frame 25 with an `ON AIR TONIGHT` red left panel. A blinking LIVE badge sits top-right throughout. The entire sequence fades from black at frame 0 and fades back to black at frame 202.

### Customization

- `NETWORK` — three-letter network abbreviation shown in the logo and corner bug (default: `NNX`)
- `SHOW_NAME` — full show title string used in the logo scene and final card (default: `NIGHTLY NEWS`)
- `DATE_LINE` — date string shown in the final card and chyron bar (default: `THURSDAY, JUNE 13`)
- `TAGLINE` — tagline rendered beneath the final title (default: `Informed. Accurate. Trusted.`)
- `ACCENT_RED` — primary brand color used for the logo glow, accent bars, chyron left panel, and LIVE dot (default: `#e8001e`)
- `GOLD` — secondary accent color used for the date separator lines and connector lines (default: `#f5c842`)
- `BG_DARK` / `BG_MID` — radial gradient stops for the main background (default: `#0a0e1a` / `#0d1421`)
- `LOCATIONS` — array of `{ label, x, y, color, delay }` objects controlling world coverage dots; `x`/`y` are percentage values of the 1280×720 canvas
- `GRID_H_COUNT` / `GRID_V_COUNT` — number of horizontal and vertical grid lines in Scene 1
