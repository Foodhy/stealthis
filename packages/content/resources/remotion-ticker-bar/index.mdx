## Scrolling Ticker Bar

This composition recreates the iconic 24-hour news channel ticker with broadcast-quality visual fidelity. The layout occupies the full 1280x720 canvas: a dark navy `#0a0e1a` background overlaid with a subtle grid and radial glows provides depth, while a simulated widescreen live-feed frame fills the center content area. The NNX logo with a red accent bar appears top-left, a blinking LIVE badge and `14:32 EST` clock sit top-right, and the show name anchors the center bottom of the overlay.

The bottom of the frame is divided into two scrolling bars. The primary **Ticker Bar** (58px tall) carries 12 breaking-news headlines separated by red `◆` diamond separators, scrolling left at `TICKER_SPEED` pixels per frame with a dark-red gradient BREAKING label on the left. Directly above it, a thinner **Market Bar** (28px) scrolls cyan-styled market tokens — S&P, DOW, NASDAQ, crude, gold, BTC, individual equities — at half the speed, prefixed by a MARKETS label. A lower-third chyron slides in at frame 55 with a spring-driven x-entrance (`damping: 18, stiffness: 110`), displaying the lead headline against a full-bleed red label block.

Four scenes structure the 450-frame runtime. The broadcast frame and bars fade in during the opening 30 frames using `interpolate` over `[0, 20]`. Info cards with viewer counts and feed stats spring up from `translateY(20px)` at frame 120. From frame 270 a spotlight dim (`rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`) descends over the live feed area, revealing a centered CONTINUOUS NEWS CRAWL label. The final scene (frame 390) springs in an NNX network stamp at `scale(0.85 → 1)` while the ticker continues uninterrupted beneath.

### Customization

- **`NETWORK_NAME`** — channel identifier displayed in the logo block (default: `"NNX"`)
- **`SHOW_NAME`** — centered show title in the broadcast overlay (default: `"NNX NIGHTLY NEWS"`)
- **`CURRENT_TIME`** — time string shown top-right (default: `"14:32 EST"`)
- **`TICKER_SPEED`** — pixels per frame for the primary news crawl (default: `2.8`; increase to scroll faster)
- **`MARKET_SPEED`** — pixels per frame for the market bar (default: `1.4`)
- **`TICKER_ITEMS`** — array of news headline strings; items starting with `◆ ` render a red diamond separator
- **`MARKET_ITEMS`** — array of market tokens and `"·"` dot separators with auto-colored deltas
- **`TICKER_HEIGHT`** / **`MARKET_BAR_HEIGHT`** — pixel heights of the two bottom bars
- **`TICKER_LABEL_WIDTH`** — width of the red BREAKING label block (default: `188px`)
- **`ACCENT_RED`** — primary brand red used on BREAKING label, chyron, separators (default: `#e8001e`)
- **`MARKET_CYAN`** — accent color for the MARKETS label and upward market moves (default: `#00d4ff`)
