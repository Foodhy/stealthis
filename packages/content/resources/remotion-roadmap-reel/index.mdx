## Roadmap Teaser Reel

A polished SaaS product roadmap animation for Remotion. The composition opens on a dark indigo canvas where a glowing gradient line draws itself from left to right over two seconds, simulating a real timeline axis. As the line extends it leaves a traveling shimmer dot at its leading edge. Four quarter markers (Q1–Q4 2025) then spring-drop onto the timeline in staggered sequence — each one triggers a feature card that slides up from below, listing two or three deliverables alongside a color-coded status pill (Done, In Progress, or Planned).

The current quarter (Q3) receives special visual treatment: its timeline dot uses the accent cyan color, emits a breathing glow ring, and its feature card uses a cyan-to-indigo gradient border so it immediately draws the viewer's eye. All earlier quarters render in a muted "completed" state while the future quarter uses reduced text opacity to signal it is not yet locked in.

The reel closes with a bold "Join Early Access" CTA button that pulses with a brand-indigo glow, paired with a secondary ghost link and a one-line social proof line. All product content (Flowbase, Q1–Q4 2025 features) is fictional but realistic — replace it at the top of the file via the `QUARTERS` array and the `C` constants object.
