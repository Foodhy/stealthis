## Firm Dashboard

A trust-first administrative overview for a law firm, built in the authoritative navy-and-gold house
style with serif headings and generous whitespace. Four KPI cards lead with active matters, billable
hours this month, collected revenue and new leads — each with a period-over-period delta.

Below, a pure-CSS bar chart compares billable hours by attorney, a leads-by-source breakdown shows
where new business originates, and a recent-matters table lists each engagement with practice area,
responsible attorney, value and a colour-coded status badge.

A month selector at the top re-bases every figure on the dashboard, and the matters table is
sortable by clicking a column header. All interactions are vanilla JavaScript with no libraries.

> Illustrative UI only — not for real legal, billing or matter-management use.
