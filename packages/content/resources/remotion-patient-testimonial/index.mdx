## Patient Testimonial Video

A polished, production-ready Remotion composition that showcases a single patient review in a vertical story format (1080 × 1920). The dark teal brand palette gives it a premium healthcare feel without relying on photographs or external assets — every visual is built from SVG shapes, CSS gradients, and CSS-in-JS color tokens.

The animation is structured as a layered sequence: a glass-morphism card fades and scales in over the first 15 frames, five star icons spring-scale one by one with an 8-frame stagger, and the quote text cascades word-by-word so viewers read along in real time. In the final act (frame 140+) a teal avatar circle and patient attribution slide in from the left with a damped spring, completing the social-proof story before the clip ends at 7 seconds.

To use this composition, copy `react.tsx` into your Remotion project (or a `src/compositions/` folder), import `RemotionRoot` from `remotion.config.ts`, and render with `npx remotion render PatientTestimonial out.mp4`. All customizable values — clinic name, colors, quote text, timing — are declared as constants at the top of the file.

### Customization

- **`CLINIC_NAME`** — replace `"Greenfield Medical"` with your clinic brand
- **`DOCTOR_NAME`** / **`PATIENT_NAME`** / **`PATIENT_INITIALS`** / **`PATIENT_SINCE`** — swap in real or test patient data
- **`QUOTE_WORDS`** — edit the array to change the testimonial text (word-by-word reveal updates automatically)
- **`STARS_START`** / **`STARS_STAGGER`** — adjust when and how fast stars appear
- **`QUOTE_START`** / **`WORDS_STAGGER`** — control quote reveal pacing
- **`AVATAR_START`** — shift the patient info entrance earlier or later
- **`SPRING_CFG`** (`damping`, `stiffness`) — tune the feel of all spring animations globally
- **Color tokens** (`BG`, `TEAL`, `TEAL_SOFT`, `WHITE`, `MUTED`) — retheme the entire composition by changing six constants
