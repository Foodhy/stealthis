## Service Highlight Video

A vertical 9:16 short-form video built entirely in Remotion that spotlights three clinic service offerings for Greenfield Medical Center. The composition runs for exactly 240 frames at 30 fps (8 seconds). Each service occupies 80 frames with its own radial glow background, a floating emoji icon in a frosted rounded tile, a bold spring-animated title that slides in from the right, and a one-line descriptor that follows a beat later. Soft ambient particles drift across the canvas tinted to the service's accent color — teal for General Checkups, coral for Lab & Diagnostics, and green for Vaccinations & Preventive Care.

Transitions between services are rendered as 10-frame cross-fades produced by `interpolate` acting on each slide's opacity. Both overlapping slides are mounted simultaneously in the React tree; only the one in its visible opacity range renders meaningful output. A persistent bottom bar carrying the clinic name anchors every frame, and a three-dot pill indicator (the active dot stretches into a capsule and glows) communicates progress to the viewer.

All motion is spring-based (`damping: 14, stiffness: 120` for content, softer springs for the card wrapper) with no CSS transitions. Every string, color, and timing constant lives at the top of the file, so the video is straightforward to rebrand or extend with a fourth service slide.

### Customization

- `CLINIC_NAME` — swap the bottom-bar text to your clinic's name
- `SERVICES` array entries — change `icon`, `title`, `description`, `accent`, and gradient colors per service
- `FRAMES_PER_SERVICE` / `CROSSFADE_FRAMES` — adjust pacing and transition length
- `DURATION_FRAMES` — total runtime; update the `<Composition>` prop to match
- `PARTICLES` array — add, remove, or adjust particle positions, sizes, and speeds
- Spring configs (`damping` / `stiffness`) in each sub-component — tune motion feel
