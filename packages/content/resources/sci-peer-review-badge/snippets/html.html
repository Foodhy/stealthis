Drift-resistant calibration of forest-canopy LiDAR using self-supervised point-cloud priors

Halvorsen, M. K.1, Adeyemi, T.1,2, Okonkwo, R.2 & Sandoval-Reyes, P.3

1 Nordreef Institute for Earth Systems · 2 Université de Meridian · 3 Cascade Polytechnic

DOI: 10.48999/jrs.2026.0334 · Received 11 Feb 2026 · Accepted 02 May 2026

Abstract. We present a calibration scheme that suppresses sensor drift in airborne LiDAR over dense canopy without ground control points. Across n = 1,284 flight lines the residual RMSE fell to 0.061 m (95% CI 0.058–0.064), a 38% reduction over the prior baseline. All data, processing code, and the registered analysis plan are openly available.
