## Billing & Subscriptions

A self-contained billing admin for **Looms Coworking**, dressed in the warm-industrial palette —
concrete neutrals, an amber accent, plant-green highlights and a matte-black sidebar. The top of the
page rolls up four live figures: monthly recurring revenue, active members, outstanding balance and
the amount collected this cycle. The MRR and member counts count up with an eased animation on load
and recompute whenever the data changes.

The members table lists each account with an avatar, plan tag, color-coded status dot, MRR and next
invoice date; past-due members carry a red **Dunning** flag. You can search by name or email and
filter by plan and status, and the footer keeps a running "filtered MRR" total. The recent-invoices
panel opens a sliding drawer with billing details, line items and tax — there you can mark an invoice
paid (which clears the member's dunning state and lifts the rollup), send a reminder, or download a
receipt. A **Change plan** dialog upgrades or downgrades any member and reports the MRR delta, and the
sidebar's dunning sweep flags every overdue account at once.

Everything is keyboard-usable and toasts confirm each action — Escape closes the drawer or dialog.

> Illustrative UI only — fictional coworking space, not a real booking system.
