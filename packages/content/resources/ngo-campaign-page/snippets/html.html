Why Loma Verde, why now

Loma Verde sits at the dry edge of the highlands, where the nearest safe water is a half-day round trip. Mothers like Inés Carral leave before dawn so their children can drink — time stolen from school, work, and rest.

Our field engineers surveyed twelve sites this spring. Each well serves around 340 people, runs on a solar pump the community is trained to maintain, and pays for itself in health and hours within a single year. Drill one well and a whole neighborhood stops getting sick from the water they need to live.

“The first well changed our mornings. Now the children walk to school, not to the river.” — Inés Carral, Loma Verde community council
