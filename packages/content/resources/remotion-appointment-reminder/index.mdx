## Appointment Reminder Clip

A polished vertical reminder clip built entirely in Remotion with zero external dependencies. The scene opens on a deep `#0a1a18` clinic background layered with three large ambient teal and coral glow orbs that breathe using `Math.sin`, plus fourteen floating dot particles that drift independently on both axes — establishing a calm, professional mood before the first element arrives. A CSS cross medical mark and clinic name fade in from above at frame 2, anchoring the brand immediately.

The animation unfolds in tight choreographed acts. The patient name "Sarah M." fades up at frame 14 beneath the greeting line, followed by a spring-bounced calendar icon that slams into view at frame 22 with a stiff spring (damping 11, stiffness 180). A coral notification dot spring-pops onto the calendar corner and then pulses continuously on a sine loop, drawing the eye. The appointment card slides up from below starting at frame 50, and its four field rows — Doctor, Specialty, Date and Time, and Location — each reveal themselves with a `translateY` + opacity spring staggered 12 frames apart (frames 62, 74, 86, 98). A teal "Upcoming" status badge scales in alongside the card. Finally the "Confirm Appointment" CTA button materialises at frame 122 with a shimmering scale-pulse that continues to the end of the clip.

All clinic name, patient name, doctor details, and color constants are declared at the top of the file — drop in your own values without touching any animation logic. The `PARTICLES` array, spring configs, and stagger offsets are isolated constants, making it straightforward to tighten or loosen the pacing.

### Customization

- `PATIENT_NAME` — swap the displayed patient name (e.g. `"John D."`)
- `DOCTOR_NAME` / `SPECIALTY` — change the provider and department
- `APPT_DATE` / `APPT_TIME` — update to a real appointment slot
- `CLINIC_NAME` — replace with your facility name
- `CTA_LABEL` — change the button copy (e.g. `"Add to Calendar"`)
- `SPRING_STD` / `SPRING_STIFF` — tweak `damping` and `stiffness` to make entrances snappier or softer
- `PARTICLES` — add or remove rows to control background density
- Card field stagger offsets (`startFrame` in `FIELDS` array) — adjust the 12-frame gaps to taste
