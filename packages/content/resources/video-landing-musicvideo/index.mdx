A landing page for a fictional music-video director, **Neon Kaskade**, built around a letterboxed cinematic hero with a scrubbing timecode, a rotating play button, and a reel of neon-lit stills. The layout leans into film language: black bars, amber accents, mono timecodes, and vibrant genre badges.

The **reel gallery** is filterable by genre (hip-hop, pop, electronic, indie) with animated card entrances and hover overlays that reveal the track, artist, and runtime. A **style-signature grid** highlights the director's visual approach, while animated stat counters tally videos shot, total streams, and awards. A scrolling **testimonial marquee** carries quotes from labels and artists.

The **booking section** is fully interactive: choosing a package, video length, and shoot date live-updates a project brief summary and an estimated budget, and submitting fires a toast confirmation. Everything is vanilla JS, keyboard-accessible, and responsive down to 360px.
