FAQ

Questions, answered (with sources, naturally)

Where do the citations come from?

Sourcely retrieves live web pages for every query, reads the relevant passages, and links each claim back to the exact page it drew from. If a fact has no source, it doesn't make the answer.

How is this different from a regular chatbot?

A chatbot answers from memory and can confidently invent facts. Sourcely reads the live web at query time and shows you the receipts, so you can verify every line in one click.

Is my search history private?

Yes. Your queries are never sold and never used to train models. On Team and Enterprise you also get SSO, audit logs and configurable data residency.

Can I use it inside my own app?

Pro and above include API access, so you can drop cited answers into your product, internal tools or Slack workspace.

What happens when sources disagree?

Sourcely shows the competing claims side by side and flags its confidence, instead of silently choosing one and hiding the rest.
