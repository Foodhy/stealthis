## Desk Booking

A self-contained desk-booking flow for a fictional coworking studio, Foundry &amp; Field. The
left panel renders a warm-concrete floor plan: fourteen desks laid out on a grid, each tinted by
its live status — green for free, amber for reserved, red for occupied — with plant accents,
window seating and phone-booth markers for context. Occupied desks are non-interactive and
announce themselves to assistive tech; free and reserved desks lift on hover and snap to a matte
black selected state.

The right rail handles the booking itself. A four-day date row and a four-option time-slot picker
(morning, afternoon, evening or full day) act as keyboard-usable radio groups. Picking a desk
opens a detail card listing its type, amenities and price in both dollars and credits. As soon as
a slot is chosen, the summary panel totals duration and cost, and the confirm button enables.

Confirming fires a toast, flips the booked desk to occupied right on the plan to simulate live
availability, and resets the selection — all in vanilla JavaScript with no frameworks or build
step. The amber-on-concrete palette, Inter type and soft shadows give it a community-studio feel,
and the layout reflows to a single column down to roughly 360px wide.

> Illustrative UI only — fictional coworking space, not a real booking system.
