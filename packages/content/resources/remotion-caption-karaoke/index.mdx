## Karaoke Caption Clip

A word-by-word karaoke caption animation designed for YouTube clips, podcast highlight reels, and social video. Each word in a sentence illuminates in yellow at its precise start frame and fades back to white when the next word begins, mimicking the classic sing-along karaoke effect. The cinematic dark background features a procedural film-grain overlay and a subtle radial vignette, giving the clip a polished, broadcast-ready look. A speaker label and episode context line float above the caption line to provide narrative grounding. All timing constants live in a single CONFIG block, making it straightforward to swap in real word-level timestamps exported from a transcription service.

## Composition specs

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1920×1080 |
| FPS | 30 |
| Duration | 600 frames (20 s) |

## Elements

- **Background** — near-black (`#0d0d0f`) with a procedural film-grain noise texture animated per-frame
- **Vignette** — radial gradient overlay darkening the corners for a cinematic feel
- **Speaker label** — "Alex Rivera" in small uppercase tracked text, fades in at frame 30
- **Episode context** — subtitle line "Episode 12 · The AI Frontier" fades in alongside the speaker label
- **Caption line** — all words rendered inline at 72 px bold `system-ui`; inactive words are white, active word transitions to `#ffd23f` (yellow) via `interpolate`
- **Word highlight transitions** — each word uses a short `interpolate` ramp for smooth color entry and exit, avoiding hard cuts between highlighted words
