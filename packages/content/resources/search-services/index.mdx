**Algolia** is the premium managed pick when search UX and speed are product differentiators.
**Meilisearch** and **Typesense** are the open-source alternatives — self-host for control or use their
cloud tiers. All three handle typos well; choose Algolia for polish and ecosystem, Meilisearch/Typesense
for cost control and ownership.
