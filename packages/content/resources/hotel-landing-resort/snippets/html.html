Our story

Born from the
light of this coast.

Azul Shores was built by the Costa family in 1998 on a private stretch of Praia da Luz. Two decades later, it remains family-run — and fiercely committed to the unhurried rhythm of this particular piece of coast.

Thirty-two rooms. A restaurant that opens at sunset. A spa carved into the cliff. And a staff-to-guest ratio that means your name will be known by breakfast.

"The kind of place you keep coming back to — not because you have to, but because nowhere else quite feels the same."
