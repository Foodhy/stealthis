Sticky-stack Sections

Scroll down — each panel pins to the top while the next one slides over it, scaling and dimming as it recedes.

Keyboard: focus the stack, then use ↓ / ↑ or PageDown / PageUp.

01

Context

Every panel is position: sticky at the top of the scroller, so it stops instead of scrolling away.

02

Constraint

The next panel keeps moving and covers the pinned one, which produces the stacked-card illusion.

03

Decision

Progress is measured per card and mapped to scale, blur and brightness so buried panels read as further back.

04

Outcome

The last panel releases normally and the document continues — no scroll trap left behind.

End of stack — normal flow resumes.
