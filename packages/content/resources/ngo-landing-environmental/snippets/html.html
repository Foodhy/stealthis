Why it works

Restoration that pays the planet back

We don't just plant — we measure survival rates, biodiversity return, and water table recovery. Transparency is the root system of trust.

🌳

94% survival rate

Native-species mixes and 3-year community stewardship keep saplings alive through their fragile first seasons.

💧

22 watersheds protected

Restored riparian buffers filter runoff and return year-round flow to streams that 90,000 people depend on.

🐦

+38% biodiversity

Camera traps and bioacoustic surveys show returning species within five years of canopy recovery.

🤝

2,900 green jobs

Local nurseries, planting crews and rangers — restoration is also an economy for the people who live there.
