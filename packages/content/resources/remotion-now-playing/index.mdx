## Now-Playing Card Animation

A 4-second Remotion animation that recreates the familiar "Now Playing" notification card with a premium dark aesthetic. The card — styled with a deep `#1e1e2e` surface, a soft purple glow border, and a subtle gradient sheen — springs up from below the frame in the first 20 frames using a damped spring, then holds steady while all its content comes alive. Track title, artist, and album are displayed in a clean typographic hierarchy using Inter at multiple weights, layered on top of 160 × 160 px gradient album art whose vinyl record overlay rotates one full degree every two frames.

The audio visualizer sits directly beside the track metadata as a row of 20 narrow bars, each driven by three stacked sine waves at different frequencies and phases — giving each bar an independent, organic pulse that convincingly mimics real spectrum activity without touching any audio API. A thin progress bar beneath the card body fills from 15 % to 45 % over the clip's duration, complete with a glowing scrubber dot and live elapsed-time counter. At frame 90, a pink heart icon scales in with a high-stiffness spring bounce — an unmistakable "liked" moment — while the pulsing play button subtly scales with a slow sine wave throughout the clip.

Floating ambient particles drift across the dark background, each with its own phase offset and a pop-in interpolation, reinforcing the music-visualizer atmosphere without cluttering the composition. All colors follow the Stealthis dark palette (purple `#a855f7`, cyan `#06b6d4`, pink `#ec4899`) with glow box-shadows and radial gradient halos to ensure every frame looks polished at 1920 × 1080.

> Simulated audio data — waveform values are generated mathematically. No real audio file is required.
