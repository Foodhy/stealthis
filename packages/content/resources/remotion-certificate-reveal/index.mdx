## Certificate Reveal

A formal certificate reveal built entirely in Remotion with zero external dependencies. The animation opens on a deep navy radial background with subtly oscillating gold dust particles drifting across the scene, establishing a prestigious atmosphere from the first frame. The certificate document — a cream-to-parchment gradient rectangle with layered box shadows — springs into existence from the center using a tuned spring (damping 14, stiffness 90) that gives it a satisfying, weighty arrival.

The SVG border traces itself in two passes: the outer 3px gold rect draws around the perimeter via `strokeDashoffset`, followed moments later by an inner decorative ring. Corner diamond ornaments, mid-edge ornament clusters, and six-point corner stars (each with their own staggered spring delay) complete the border treatment. Ribbon banners slide in from the left and right edges on their own springs and gently float in a continuous sine loop. The recipient name types in character by character via a frame-to-substring index, complete with a blinking cursor. A rotating serrated-ring gold seal — built entirely from SVG lines — springs up from the bottom-right corner with a slight overshoot and then pulses softly. The scene ends with a single diagonal shimmer sweep across the entire frame.

All content (recipient name, award title, issuer, signatories, date) is declared as constants at the top of the file — swap any value without touching animation logic.
