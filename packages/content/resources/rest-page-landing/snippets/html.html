Our story

A garden, a fire, a table for thirty.

Casa Olivar opened in 2017 in a converted carriage house on Calle del Olivar. The kitchen is built around a single wood-fired hearth and a half-hectare garden in Aravaca that supplies the herbs, tomatoes, and figs from May through October.

Our menu changes weekly with what the garden gives us. The constants are a hand-cut pasta, a whole grilled fish, and a dry-aged steak — finished with whatever herb is loudest that morning.
