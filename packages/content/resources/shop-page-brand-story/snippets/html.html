Our mission

Make the everyday feel intentional.

Mass production taught us to expect less from the objects we live with. We wanted the opposite — pieces you notice, repair, and pass on. Every Lumen & Field product starts with a question: will this still feel good to own in ten years?

We answer it with slow materials, small batches, and a workshop that knows every maker by name. No outsourced anonymity, no planned obsolescence — just light, warmth, and craft you can stand behind.

The journey

A decade, milestone by milestone

  1. 2014

    One candle, one table

    Founder Mara Quinn pours the first 40 candles by hand and sells out at a Saturday market.

  2. 2017

    The first workshop

    A 600 sq ft studio opens. We hire three makers and add ceramics to the line.

  3. 2020

    Closing the loop

    We launch refill jars and a take-back program — 90% less packaging waste per order.

  4. 2024

    Carbon-neutral, certified

    Every shipment offsets its footprint. The repair pledge becomes lifetime, for good.
