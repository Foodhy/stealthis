## Quote Short Video

A full-screen vertical short (1080×1920) designed for social media reels and stories. An oversized decorative opening quote mark fades and scales into view, then the quote text animates in word by word using spring physics. A horizontal gradient accent line draws in from the left, followed by the author attribution sliding up from below. A branded footer fades in near the end, and the composition holds for a clean 4-second pause before looping. The deep navy background features a subtle radial purple glow for visual depth.
