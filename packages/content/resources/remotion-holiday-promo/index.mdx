## Holiday Promo

A cinematic 5-second holiday promo built entirely in Remotion. Six snowflake particles drift downward at varying speeds and horizontal offsets using `interpolate` on their Y position. The headline "Happy Holidays" types itself character by character with a per-character stagger, and a golden glow behind the text grows in sync. A gift box assembled from nested divs plays a spring-powered lid-lift animation that starts mid-clip.

The offer badge ("25% off everything") fades and slides in with staggered lines after the gift box begins unwrapping. Everything fades to black in the final 20 frames. Customizable constants at the top of the file let you swap the headline text, promo code, discount amount, and color palette without touching the animation logic.

Use this as an opener card in a holiday email campaign video, a social reel bumper, or as a Remotion template starter for seasonal promos. Adjust `DURATION_FRAMES` if you need a longer loop.
