Pick auth by how much you want managed vs. controlled. **Clerk** is the fastest path for modern React/Next.js
with polished UI. **Auth0** and **WorkOS** cover enterprise SSO and complex identity. **Supabase Auth** shines
when you're already on Supabase Postgres with RLS. **Better Auth** and **Auth.js** are open-source if you
want to self-host. **Kinde** adds orgs and RBAC with a generous free tier. **Stytch** for passwordless-first
flows. For full backends (DB + auth + storage), see [Backend as a Service](/r/backend-as-a-service) too.
