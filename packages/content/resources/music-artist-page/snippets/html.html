    About

    Neon Tides is the synth-pop project of fictional songwriter Mara Vel, trading in widescreen choruses, glassy arpeggios, and late-night reverb. Since the breakout single Paper Lanterns, the act has grown from bedroom demos into sold-out neon-soaked rooms, scoring its sound somewhere between coastal nostalgia and city static.
