## Market Watch Ticker

A ten-second Remotion composition styled for a premium financial news broadcast. The entire 300-frame sequence unfolds across a deep navy background (`#0a0e18`) with a radial blue glow anchored at the top center, a faint dot-grid texture at 3% opacity, and a subtle cyan ambient glow at the bottom — layered to give the stage real depth before any content appears.

Scene 1 opens on frame 0 with the NNX Markets branded header springing down from above (`damping: 20, stiffness: 130`). The header carries a blue-gradient NNX logo block, a red pulsing LIVE badge, and a green NYSE OPEN status indicator, all on a single row separated by a cyan-tipped horizontal rule that scales in from the left. Immediately beneath, three index pills — S&P 500 +1.24%, DOW +0.87%, NASDAQ +1.56% — slide up together with a follow-on spring at frame 18. Scene 2 begins at frame 50 when six stock cards start staggering in (12-frame spacing per card), filling a 3×2 grid. Each card has a colored top-accent bar, ticker symbol, company name, large monospace price, a change line, a color-coded percentage badge, and a seven-point SVG sparkline that draws itself from left to right via an Easing.out(cubic) interpolation between frames 10 and 55 relative to the card's delay. Positive stocks use `#22c55e`, negative stocks use `#e8001e`.

At frame 200 a dark red headline ticker bar slides up from the bottom and reveals itself over 20 frames. The MARKETS label badge sits in a solid `#e8001e` block on the left; the scrolling text — "FED SIGNALS POTENTIAL RATE CUT · OIL PRICES SURGE 3.2% · GOLD HITS 6-MONTH HIGH · …" — translates left via an `Easing.linear` interpolate across the ticker's 90-frame lifetime. The main content group fades out at frame 255 and is replaced by the Scene 4 outro: a centered composition with a spring-driven headline ("S&P 500 closes at 5,234.18"), a fading sub-line, and the channel watermark in cyan — all fading to the base background by frame 300.

### Customization

- `CHANNEL_NAME` — the on-screen network name shown in the outro watermark
- `MARKET_STATUS` — text in the green NYSE OPEN badge (e.g. `"NASDAQ OPEN"`)
- `MARKET_SUMMARY` — array of `{ index, change, positive }` objects for the three top-line index pills
- `STOCKS` — six-entry array of `{ symbol, name, price, change, changePct, positive, sparkline[] }` driving the card grid; swap prices, names and sparkline shapes to match any market snapshot
- `HEADLINE_TICKER` — single string of scrolling news copy; separate items with ` · ` separators
- `OUTRO_HEADLINE` and `OUTRO_SUB` — closing text rendered in the Scene 4 summary
- `ACCENT_GREEN`, `ACCENT_RED`, `ACCENT_CYAN` — broadcast palette primaries; change these to rebrand for a different network color scheme
- `BG_COLOR` — base background hex; defaults to `#0a0e18` (deep navy)
