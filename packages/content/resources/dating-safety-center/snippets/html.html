Safety tips

6 tips

Scammers push to move to text or WhatsApp fast. Stay in Sparkle chat until you truly trust someone — our filters flag suspicious links and requests here.

A quick 5-minute video chat confirms your match looks like their photos and helps you read the vibe before committing to an in-person date.

Share where you're going and who you're meeting. Use Sparkle's live location share so a trusted friend can check in on you.

Pick a busy café, bar, or park for a first date. Skip private homes or remote spots until you've met a few times and feel comfortable.

Arrange your own way there and back so you can leave whenever you want. Keep your phone charged and don't leave drinks unattended.

Never share your home address, workplace, or financial info early on. No genuine match will ever ask you to send money or gift cards.

Community guidelines

Use recent photos of yourself and honest info. Impersonation, catfishing, and fake profiles get removed permanently.

No harassment, hate speech, or unsolicited explicit content. Treat every match the way you'd want to be treated.

No solicitation, scams, or requests for money. Report anyone who pressures you off-platform or asks for financial help.

Sparkle is strictly for adults. Accounts belonging to minors are banned and reported to the appropriate authorities.
