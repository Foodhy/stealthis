## Sale Announcement Spot

A high-energy 3-second sale announcement animation built for product drops, flash sales, and promotional reels. The composition layers a radial glow background with a cinematic depth stack: confetti particles scatter behind the central badge, while the pricing elements arrive sequentially to drive the viewer's eye from discount percentage down to the new price.

The **SALE** title explodes in with a spring that overshoots to 1.2× before settling at 1×, creating an instant visual punch. The **50% OFF** badge slides in from the left edge with an elastic spring, followed by a fading original price with a red strikethrough, and finally the new price bouncing down from above. All elements are wrapped in a global fade-out over the last 20 frames for a clean exit.

Key constants are all defined at the top of the file — swap `BRAND`, `DISCOUNT_PCT`, `ORIGINAL_PRICE`, `NEW_PRICE`, and `ACCENT_COLOR` to adapt this spot to any campaign in seconds.
