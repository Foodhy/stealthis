Geochemistry · Letter

Self-organizing manganese dendrites record sub-annual climate oscillations in cave speleothems

M. Okonkwo-Reyes, L. Hartwell, S. Nakamura, F. Ben Salah & J. Voss

Layered manganese-oxide dendrites within a Pleistocene flowstone preserve a periodic banding consistent with a 4.2-month forcing cycle. Using synchrotron micro-XRF mapping across n = 1 482 growth bands, we reconstruct a 9 600-year record of monsoon variability and propose a coupled redox–transport mechanism for spontaneous pattern formation.
