02 / ABOUT

I’m a product designer who treats constraints like a feature. I’ve spent eleven years stripping interfaces down to their load-bearing parts — then drawing a thick black box around them.

I work fast, document everything, and ship. I believe good design doesn’t need to whisper. I’ve led design at two startups, mentored a dozen juniors, and given talks on systems thinking from Berlin to Nairobi.
