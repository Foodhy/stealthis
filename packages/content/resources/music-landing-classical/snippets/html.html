    Artist Spotlight

    Élise Maron

    Music Director & Conductor

    In her fourth season at the helm, Maron has reshaped the orchestra's voice — luminous, unhurried, fearless in its quiet. Trained in Vienna and Lyon, she pairs the canon with living composers, insisting that every programme hold a question as well as an answer.

    “An orchestra is eighty-four people agreeing to listen harder than they speak.”

    The Hall

    Aurelian Hall

    Opened in 1903 and acoustically restored in 2018, the Aurelian Hall seats 1,460 beneath a gilded dome. Its shoebox geometry gives the strings a warmth that recording engineers travel continents to capture.

    • Capacity 1,460 seats
    • Address 14 Meridian Crescent
    • Doors 45 minutes before curtain
    • Access Step-free throughout
