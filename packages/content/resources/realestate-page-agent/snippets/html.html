About

Quietly relentless, endlessly local.

For seventeen years I have helped families buy and sell along the Marisol coastline — from cliff‑edge moderns to restored bungalows three streets back from the water. My practice is small by design: fewer clients, deeper attention, sharper results.

Whether it is your first home or your fifth, you will always work directly with me — never a handoff.
