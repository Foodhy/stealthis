Frequently asked

Before you write to us.

Deposit and cancellation

We hold the date with a 25% deposit, charged on signature of the quote. Free cancellation up to 14 days before the event; 50% refund between 14 and 7 days; non-refundable inside 7 days.

Allergies and dietary requirements

We design a menu that accommodates all confirmed dietary needs at no extra cost — please send the full list 7 days before service. Gluten, dairy, nut and shellfish substitutions are routine.

Audio, projection and decoration

The room ships with a bluetooth audio system, two cordless mics, and a 65" wall-mounted display. Floral & printed decoration are welcome with 48 h drop-off; no open flame.

Off-site catering radius

We service venues within 40 km of Madrid centre. Beyond that, we can partner with a local kitchen — get in touch and we'll see what's possible.
