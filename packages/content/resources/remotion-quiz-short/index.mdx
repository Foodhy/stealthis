## Quiz Short Video

An interactive-style quiz short designed for social media reels and YouTube Shorts. The composition opens with a JavaScript trivia question sliding in from the top, then reveals four multiple-choice answer options one by one with spring animations. A countdown timer bar at the top ticks down while viewers "think," followed by a dramatic countdown ring pulsing around the correct answer before a green flash confirms it. The finale drops a "CORRECT: object" banner onto the screen while 20 colorful confetti dots spring outward from center — ideal for dev-education creators, coding bootcamps, or any brand running engagement-driven quiz content on vertical platforms.
