CSS Scroll-Driven Animations

Elements that animate to the beat of your scroll.

Every motion below is declared in CSS and bound to a scroll() or view() timeline — no scroll listeners, no requestAnimationFrame. Scroll down to see each technique labeled and explained.

01 · view()

Reveal on enter

Each card owns an animation-timeline: view(). The keyframes play across the animation-range as the card crosses the viewport — driven by geometry, not time.

02 · scroll()

Progress from scroll()

The top bar and this dial share the anonymous scroll() timeline of the page root. Their fill is a keyframe animation scrubbed 0→100% by the scroll position.

scroll(root block) Animation is paused; scroll advances its timeline.
03 · stagger

Staggered rows

Same view() timeline, but each row nudges its animation-range start a little later, so they cascade in as the group scrolls up.

    04 · parallax

    Layered parallax

    One view() timeline drives three layers at different translate rates over the cover range — depth without a single scroll event.
