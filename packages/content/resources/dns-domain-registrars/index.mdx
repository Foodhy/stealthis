Buy your domain once, point DNS at your host. **Cloudflare** for at-cost domains with CDN and SSL
included — pairs with [Hosting & Deploy](/r/hosting-deploy). **Namecheap** and **Porkbun** for
budget-friendly registration with privacy. **Google Domains** (Squarespace) for a simple panel
if you're in the Google ecosystem.
