## Two-column CV

A balanced, modern resume layout split into a dark left sidebar and a light right
main column. The sidebar carries the identity — a CSS-drawn avatar, contact lines,
animated proficiency bars, dot-rated languages, and social links — while the main
column reads top to bottom: summary, a vertical experience timeline with date pills,
education cards, and a grid of selected-project tiles.

A frosted floating toolbar drives the interactions. Five accent swatches re-tint the
whole page through CSS custom properties (indigo, emerald, rose, amber, slate), a
density toggle switches between comfortable and compact spacing for fitting more on a
page, and a print button opens the browser print dialog. Accent and density choices
persist in `localStorage`, the swatch group is keyboard-navigable with arrow keys, and
clicking the email copies it to the clipboard.

Dedicated print CSS keeps both columns side by side on paper, preserves the accent
colors, and avoids breaking project and timeline blocks across pages — so the on-screen
CV and the exported PDF stay in sync.
