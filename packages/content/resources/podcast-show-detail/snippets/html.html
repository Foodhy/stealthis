About the show

Every Thursday, Signal & Static pulls apart one idea from the frontier of audio technology — from spatial sound and generative music to the tiny engineering decisions that make a voice feel human. Mara and Dev sit down with the researchers, founders, and tinkerers actually shipping this work.

Expect long-form, unhurried conversations: no hype cycles, no press-release readouts. Just curious people asking better questions. Recorded live in Lisbon and streamed to a community of nearly two million weekly listeners across the globe.
