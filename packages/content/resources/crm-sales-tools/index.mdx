**HubSpot** when you want CRM plus marketing in one platform with a free tier. **Pipedrive** for
visual sales pipelines. **Attio** for a modern, flexible data model that syncs from email.
**Folk** for a lightweight spreadsheet-feel CRM for small teams. Pair with
[Customer Support Tools](/r/customer-support-tools) for post-sale and [Forms & Surveys](/r/forms-surveys)
for lead capture.
