The Verdant Empire

The Verdant Empire (fl. 412–1089) was a confederated agrarian state spanning the river basins of the central continent. Renowned for its terraced hydro-gardens and the Codex Viridis, the empire pioneered a system of canal-borne courier networks that linked over four hundred chartered townships. Its decline followed the Great Silt Drought, after which the ruling Council of Stewards dissolved into rival city-leagues. At its height the empire's grain reserves were said to feed a population of nine million across three climatic zones. (Full article…)
