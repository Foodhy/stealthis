How it works

Three small steps, at your pace. No pressure, no paperwork to wrestle with.

  1. 01

    Say hello

    Book a free 15-minute intro call. We’ll listen, answer your questions, and there’s no obligation to continue.

  2. 02

    Find your fit

    We match you with a therapist suited to what you’re facing — and you choose in person or online.

  3. 03

    Begin, gently

    Start your first session whenever you’re ready. Pause, change therapist or step away any time.

Our approach

Care that meets you where you are

We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all. Our therapists draw on CBT, person-centred and trauma-informed practice — but more than any method, we believe healing happens in a relationship that feels safe.

Talk to us first
  • Safety first A confidential space, free of judgement, always at your pace.
  • You lead You set the goals. We walk alongside, we don’t push.
  • Whole-person Your story, body and context — not just a symptom list.
  • No rush Stay as long as helps, step away whenever you need to.

If you need help right now

Willowmere isn’t an emergency service. If you’re in crisis or worried about your safety, please call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or your local emergency number — help is available 24/7, and reaching out is a brave thing to do.
