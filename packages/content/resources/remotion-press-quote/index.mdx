## Press Quote Card

This Remotion composition delivers an elegant, cinematic press quote card across four distinct scenes at 1280×720, 30 fps. It opens with a large typographic open-quote character (`"`) rendered in Georgia serif at 140px, springing in from scale 0 at the top-left of the card area using a tight `damping: 16, stiffness: 200` spring — accompanied by a red drop-shadow glow. Immediately after, a three-pixel horizontal accent line sweeps from left to right using a cubic ease-out interpolation, establishing the composition's broadcast authority. Corner bracket accents and a vertical left-edge gradient bar also animate in during this opening window, reinforcing the premium card frame.

The core of the composition (frames 30–90) is the quote text reveal: the seventeen words of the attributed quote each fade in independently, staggered three frames apart. Each word starts at 8px below its resting position and translates upward with a quadratic ease-out as it reaches full opacity — creating a flowing, almost teleprompter-like read that feels authored rather than automated. Above the quote, a red `OPINION` pill badge and a broadcast dateline slide in from the left at frame 18, giving the card a proper chyron identity. The background uses a `#0d1117` deep charcoal with subtle grid lines and a radial vignette for broadcast-studio depth.

From frame 90, the attribution block springs up from 28px below using `damping: 20, stiffness: 140`, revealing the speaker name in full white and the title in muted grey — separated by a red em-dash. A 200px cyan separator line sweeps in simultaneously. At frame 100 a red pill badge reading `NNX EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW` scales in from the right edge using a fast `damping: 18, stiffness: 180` spring. The closing scene (frames 130–150) introduces the NNX logo bar at the bottom — a red circle logo mark, show name, and a `LIVE BROADCAST` label — while a mirrored closing-quote mark springs in from the bottom-right and fades to 45% opacity for a typographic echo effect.

### Customization

- `QUOTE` — the full pull-quote text, split word-by-word for the staggered reveal animation
- `SPEAKER` — attributed person's full name displayed in white bold after the em-dash
- `SPEAKER_TITLE` — official title displayed in muted grey beside the speaker name
- `SOURCE_BADGE` — text inside the red pill badge on the right (default: NNX EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW)
- `NETWORK` — two-to-four letter network call sign shown inside the red circle logo mark
- `SHOW_NAME` — full show name displayed next to the logo mark in the bottom bar
- `ACCENT_RED` — primary red used for the quote mark, accent line, badge, logo, and corner accents (default: #e8001e)
- `ACCENT_CYAN` — cyan color for the attribution em-dash and separator line (default: #00d4ff)
- `BG` — main background color (default: #0d1117)
