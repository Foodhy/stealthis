Not sure what to learn next? These resources give you **structured paths** instead of an endless list of tutorials.

**roadmap.sh** is the go-to for a bird's-eye view — pick a role (frontend, backend, DevOps, etc.) and follow the visual map with linked resources. **freeCodeCamp** and **MDN Learn** are better when you want hands-on exercises and a linear curriculum starting from zero.
