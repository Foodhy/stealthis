Good to know

Questions, answered

Does treatment hurt?

You may feel mild pressure for a day or two after adjustments or new aligner trays — that's your smile moving into place. Over-the-counter relief and soft foods handle it easily.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

For mild to moderate cases, absolutely. Complex bite corrections sometimes respond faster to braces. Your free scan tells us which path gives you the best result.

How much does it really cost?

Plans start at $2,900. Most patients split it across 0% APR monthly payments. Use the estimator above to preview yours — the quote at your consult is fixed, with no surprises.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes. Many plans cover a portion of orthodontic care. We verify your benefits during your consult and apply them before financing the balance.
