This orthodontics landing page opens with a reassuring hero — a confident value promise, trust badges, and a compact free-consultation form that validates inline and confirms with a toast. Below it, a side-by-side comparison lets visitors weigh traditional braces against clear aligners, with a segmented toggle that swaps highlighted specs (visibility, treatment time, visit cadence, and price band) for each option.

A four-step process timeline walks patients from free scan to retention, and a financing panel turns cost anxiety into clarity: pick a treatment plan, drag the monthly-budget slider, and watch the estimated term, monthly payment, and total recalculate live against a 0% APR in-house plan. An expandable FAQ and a closing call-to-action round out the flow, all keyboard-navigable with visible focus states and a responsive layout that collapses gracefully on small screens.

Every control is powered by vanilla JavaScript — the comparison toggle, financing math, FAQ accordion, smooth-scroll anchors, and form handling — with no external libraries.
