## Event Promo Teaser

A cinematic 5-second (150-frame) conference promo designed for social media teasers, event pages, and pre-roll ads. The composition opens with a dark navy background (#0f172a) lit by two large radial glows — tech blue on the left and purple on the right — that breathe gently throughout. Eight small glowing dot particles drift across the scene in independent sinusoidal arcs, layering atmospheric depth behind all foreground content.

The event title "DEVCONF 2026" springs into view at full scale around frame 0–18, styled with a white-to-blue-to-purple gradient and a one-time shimmer sweep that crosses the letters by frame 55. A gradient divider line expands outward beneath the title. From frame 35 the date and location appear as icon-accented rounded pills sliding up from 40px below their final position. Three speaker avatar circles (colored-gradient placeholders with initials) cascade in with staggered springs from frame 55. A horizontal divider precedes the "Register Now" CTA button, which springs in at frame 80 and then pulses its glow for the remainder of the clip. The last 20 frames fade the entire composition to black.

All design values are top-level `UPPERCASE` constants: `EVENT_NAME`, `EVENT_DATES`, `EVENT_LOCATION`, `CTA_TEXT`, `COLOR_BLUE`, `COLOR_PURPLE`, and the `SPEAKERS` array. Swap these to adapt the template to any event in under a minute.
