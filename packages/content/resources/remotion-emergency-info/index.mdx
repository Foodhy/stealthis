## Emergency Info Card

A high-urgency information card built entirely in Remotion — no external assets, no CSS files, no Google Fonts. The deep `#0a1a18` background is dominated by a `#d4503e` danger-red radial glow that breathes slowly via a sine wave, immediately signalling an emergency context. Layered scan-line texture and an edge vignette add cinematic tension without cluttering the message hierarchy. A pulsing alert circle springs in at frame 0 with a stiff spring (damping 11, stiffness 180), scaling from zero and then continuously oscillating between 0.95× and 1.05× via `Math.sin` — creating a lifelike heartbeat that keeps the viewer's attention anchored.

The "Emergency?" headline enters from below using a standard spring (damping 14, stiffness 120) in DANGER red with a live text-glow that pulses in sync. Below it, an "Act Immediately" pill badge flashes at a rapid `Math.abs(Math.sin)` cadence for maximum urgency. The three emergency contact lines — Emergency Room, Emergency Line, and Ambulance — each slide in from the left with a spring offset by 15 frames, emerging from behind a coral left-border accent bar whose glow also breathes continuously. All data and clinic branding is declared as top-level constants so the card can be rebranded in seconds.

### Customization

- `CLINIC_NAME` — swap the clinic display name at the top of the card
- `EMERGENCY_HEADLINE` — change the main headline text
- `EMERGENCY_LINES` array — update icon emoji, label, and contact detail for each row
- `FOOTER_TEXT` — adjust the small disclaimer/contact text at the bottom
- `LINE_START_FRAME` — push the first line reveal earlier or later in the timeline
- `STAGGER_FRAMES` — increase for slower stagger, decrease for rapid-fire reveal
- `SPRING_STD` / `SPRING_STIFF` — tune damping and stiffness to soften or sharpen all transitions
- `DANGER`, `CORAL`, `MUTED` — remap danger-palette colors to any brand scheme
