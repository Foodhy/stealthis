Amara & Julian

A little tale of coffee spilled, trains missed, and a promise made under September lamplight. Here is how the two of us found our forever.

Saturday, the twelfth of September · Willowmere Gardens

Chapter One

How We Met

  1. 2019 · Spring

    A Spilled Latte

    We reached for the same corner table at Marlowe & Co. Julian knocked my flat white across my notebook. He bought the replacement; I kept the ruined pages.

  2. 2019 · Autumn

    The Missed Train

    Six months of almost-dates until we both missed the 8:14 to Brighton. We walked the whole platform and talked until the next one, then let that one go too.

  3. 2021 · Summer

    A Key, A Kettle

    We painted a tiny flat the color of oat milk and argued happily over bookshelves. The kettle whistled us awake every morning after.

  4. 2024 · September

    The Question

    Under a lamppost in Willowmere Gardens, Julian forgot his speech and simply asked. Amara said yes before he finished the sentence.
