## Integration Showcase

A 10-second Remotion composition built for SaaS product videos, landing page headers, and conference decks. The scene opens with the Flowbase product logo fading and scaling into the center, surrounded by a pulsing double-ring glow that breathes throughout the entire clip. A faint dashed orbit circle materializes around it, signalling the space where integrations will appear. Eight integration cards — each a rounded-square tile carrying a brand-colored symbol, background, and name label — fly in one by one from their respective compass directions using `spring()` physics. Every card travels from 500px beyond the orbit ring and decelerates naturally into its slot, creating a satisfying staggered cascade.

Once all eight cards have settled, animated connection lines extend outward from the product logo toward each integration simultaneously. The lines use a `linear-gradient` drawn by growing their CSS `width` on each frame, giving the impression of data flowing from the hub out to every spoke. The background features a layered radial gradient: a deep indigo bloom at the center with a wider cyan ambient ring, both modulated by a slow sinusoidal pulse so the composition never feels static.

The final act begins at frame 230: a counter springs up from 0 to 500+ with a large bold typeface, followed by an indigo pill CTA reading "Explore integrations →". Both elements slide in from slightly below with opacity fades, keeping the reveal hierarchy clear. The entire scene fades to black over the last 15 frames. All styling is pure inline React — no external CSS, fonts, or assets required.
