Neon Ronin

Story & Art by Mika Tendo · Letters by R. Okafor

  • Action
  • Cyberpunk
  • Sci-Fi
  • Drama

In the rain-soaked sprawl of Neo-Kanazawa, the last free swordsmith forges blades that cut through firewalls as easily as flesh. When the megacorp Helix Dynamics brands the wandering mercenary Kaze a domestic threat, she trades her severance chip for a stolen katana and a debt she can never repay. Hunted across thirteen districts, she discovers the corporation's deepest secret is wired straight into her own spine — and that every duel she wins rewrites a memory she didn't know she'd lost. Loyalty is a subscription, honor is contraband, and the only thing the city can't pirate is a clean death.
