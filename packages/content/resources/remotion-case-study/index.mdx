## Case Study Highlight

A premium four-act case study video built entirely in Remotion with inline React styles and no external animation libraries. The composition tells a complete B2B success story arc — from the pain point through the solution to hard numbers and a closing call-to-action — all in 15 seconds at a cinematic pace suitable for landing pages, sales decks, and LinkedIn video ads.

Each act uses spring-based entrances for organic motion: the company logo scales in with an overshoot, solution cards slide from the left with staggered delays, and the three metric cards each count their numbers up from zero using Remotion's `spring()` function. The transition between acts is a smooth cross-fade driven by `interpolate()` ranges, so acts overlap slightly and the viewer never sees a hard cut. A global radial glow pulse underneath adds depth without distracting from the content.

All copy, metrics, and brand colours are defined as uppercase constants at the top of the file — swap `COMPANY_NAME`, `PRODUCT`, `METRICS`, and the palette tokens to rebrand the entire video in under two minutes.
