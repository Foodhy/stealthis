Fan info

Everything you need before kickoff. Tap a question to expand.

What time do gates open?

Turnstiles open 90 minutes before kickoff at 18:15. We recommend arriving early as the Harbor Derby draws large crowds and security checks are in place at all entrances.

How do I get to Harbor Stadium?

The North Dock tram stops directly outside the South Family stand. Park-and-ride is available at Pier 7 with shuttles every 10 minutes. There is no public parking at the stadium on matchdays.

What is the bag policy?

Bags larger than A4 (210×297mm) are not permitted. A free bag-drop is available near the East Stand. Sealed water bottles up to 500ml are allowed.

Are tickets digital?

Yes — all tickets are issued as a QR code in the TixGate app. You can transfer a ticket to a friend up to two hours before kickoff. No printouts are accepted at the turnstiles.
