On the Cover

p. 48

The Year the River Ran Backward

For a century the town of Verano measured its fortunes by the river. Now the engineers measure something else — and the people who stayed behind are learning a vocabulary nobody asked for: drawdown, dead pool, the slow arithmetic of a basin that no longer fills. A dispatch from the far edge of the drought.

By Marcus Halloran · Photographs by Daniela Ferreira · 24 min read

“You don’t notice a river leaving. You notice the silence where it used to be.”
