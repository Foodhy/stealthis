## Animated Area Chart

A Remotion composition that visualizes weekly revenue data as a smooth area chart with cinematic sequencing. The stroke line is drawn first using a horizontal clip-rect that sweeps from left to right over 80 frames, tracing a catmull-rom cubic-bezier path through 12 data points. A split second later, a second clip-rect follows and reveals the gradient fill beneath — transitioning from indigo at 42% opacity at the crest down to fully transparent at the baseline.

Twelve dot markers spring in one by one along the finished line, each bouncing into place with configurable damping. The final data point receives a special callout badge: a cyan-outlined pill connected to the dot by a dashed stem, displaying the current week's revenue as a count-up number that animates from zero to `$124,800`. A subtle dashed vertical rule highlights the position of that final point. The composition closes with four summary statistics — peak week, average, quarter-over-quarter growth, and total Q1 revenue — sliding up from the bottom in a staggered spring sequence.

The dark background (`#0a0a0f`) pairs with a soft indigo radial glow centered on the chart body and a secondary cyan accent near the top-right, giving the composition the feel of a live dashboard rather than a static slide. Grid lines and axis labels fade in alongside the title header to orient the viewer before the data motion begins.

### Data format

The chart reads from the `DATA` constant at the top of the file — an array of `{ week: string; value: number }` objects. Swap in any 6–12 data points and adjust `maxVal` in the layout constants to match your data ceiling. The summary stats row at the bottom is also hardcoded; update those four objects (`label`, `value`, `color`) to reflect the derived numbers from your dataset. The `COMPANY` and `PERIOD` strings at the top control the header copy.
