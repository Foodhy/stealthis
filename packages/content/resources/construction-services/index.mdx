## Construction Services

A sturdy, trades-flavoured marketing page for a general contractor. The hero pairs a chunky
condensed headline with a hi-vis yellow estimate button and a diagonal hazard accent strip. Below it,
a row of filter chips lets visitors narrow the service grid by trade — **New Build**, **Remodel**,
**Additions**, **Concrete**, **Roofing**, **Electrical** and **Plumbing** — each card carrying an
icon, a short blurb and a typical lead-time badge.

A four-step process timeline walks clients through **Consult → Estimate → Build → Handover**, and a
final CTA band invites them to request a free estimate with an inline form that validates and confirms
without leaving the page. Filtering, the live result count and the form are all handled in vanilla JS.

The steel-and-concrete palette, bold borders and hazard diagonals give it the worksite feel without
sacrificing accessible headings, labels and focus styles.

> Illustrative UI only — not for real construction or contracting use.
