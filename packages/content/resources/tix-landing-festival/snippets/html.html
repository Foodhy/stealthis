Good to Know

The essentials before you head to the desert.

Is there an age limit?

SOLSTICE is all-ages. Under-16s enter free with a ticketed adult. The Mirage Club afterparties are 18+.

Can I upgrade my pass later?

Yes — log into your wallet anytime before Aug 1 and pay the difference to move from GA to VIP, subject to availability.

What can I bring into the grounds?

Empty refillable bottles, sunscreen, a small bag, and a printed or wallet ticket. No glass, drones, or pro cameras.

How does camping work?

Camping passes include a numbered 10×10 ft pitch. Bring your own tent, or rent a pre-pitched bell tent as a checkout add-on.

What's the refund policy?

All passes are non-refundable but fully transferable. Add ticket protection at checkout for a flexible cancellation window.
