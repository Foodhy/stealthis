## Product Tour Video

A three-screen product walkthrough rendered entirely in Remotion. The composition simulates a real SaaS app called "Launchpad" and is split into 5-second segments — Dashboard, Settings, and Reports — each entering with a spring-driven fade-zoom transition and exiting with a complementary zoom-out fade. A row of progress dots at the top and a "Step N of 3" footer keep viewers oriented throughout.

Every screen uses a distinct animation vocabulary. The Dashboard segment opens with a browser chrome mock containing a sidebar and three KPI cards (MRR, DAU, Churn) that spring up sequentially, followed by a smooth SVG line chart that draws itself left-to-right using stroke-dashoffset, capped with a labeled callout arrow pointing to the MRR peak. The Settings segment reveals a config panel with staggered form rows, then spotlights an active automation toggle with a pulsing glow and a callout. The Reports segment grows four quarterly revenue bars upward using eased interpolation, then staggers five table rows in from the right, with a callout directing attention to the CSV export button.

Design stays consistent across all three screens: `#0a0a0f` deep background, `#1a1a2e` card surfaces, indigo/violet brand colors, a cyan accent, and a radial vignette overlay. All product data is fictional but realistic — swap the `PRODUCT` constant and data arrays at the top of `react.tsx` to brand it for any SaaS.
