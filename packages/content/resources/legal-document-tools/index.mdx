Every SaaS needs Terms and a Privacy Policy before launch. **Termly** for auto-updated docs plus
consent banners (see [Privacy & Consent Tools](/r/privacy-consent-tools)). **Iubenda** for
multi-language compliance across EU markets. **TermsFeed** for free basic templates. **GetTerms**
for a simple one-time purchase. Not legal advice — use these as starting points and consult a
lawyer for your jurisdiction.
