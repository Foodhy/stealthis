The Raft Consensus Protocol

Raft is a consensus algorithm designed to be more understandable than Paxos. It decomposes the problem into leader election, log replication and safety, and underpins the replication layer of Aurora DB. A single elected leader accepts client writes, appends them to a durable log, and replicates entries to follower nodes; an entry commits once a majority acknowledge it.
