About

Built in basements, broadcast to skylines

NOVA RAIN started as a bedroom experiment with a broken drum machine and a refusal to sleep. Three albums later, the project has become a touring force — fusing club-ready low end with widescreen synth melodies that feel built for the last train home.

Every release is self-produced, every show hand-mixed. The mission stays the same: turn the noise of the night into something you can dance to.
