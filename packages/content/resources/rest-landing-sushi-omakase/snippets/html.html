店について

About the bar

Eight seats. One chef.

Kuro is an eight-seat counter that opens twice a night. Chef Kenji trained for nine years in Ginza before moving to New York in 2018. The bar is hinoki cypress, the knives are forged in Sakai, and the tea is sourced from a single farm in Uji.
