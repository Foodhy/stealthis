**Framer** when prototypes should become live sites — also listed under
[Design Software](/r/design-software) as a full design canvas. **ProtoPie** for complex mobile
interactions with sensors and logic. **Maze** and **Useberry** to validate designs with real users —
Maze for end-to-end research at scale (panel, interviews, AI reports), Useberry for fast unmoderated
tests on Figma or ProtoPie prototypes and live sites. **Miro** for collaborative UX workshops,
journey mapping, and IA (try the [Effective UX Workshop](https://miro.com/templates/the-effective-ux-workshop/)
template on Miroverse). **Principle** for quick motion studies from Figma/Sketch. For web motion in
code, see [Web Animation Libraries](/r/web-animation-libraries).
