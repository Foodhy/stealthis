Charred Tomato & Saffron Orzo

A one-pan supper that tastes like a long Sunday but cooks in under forty minutes. Blistered cherry tomatoes melt into a saffron-stained broth, the orzo drinks it all up, and a shower of lemon and basil pulls it bright at the end.

Method

  1. 1

    Warm the olive oil in a wide, deep pan over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and saffron and let them sizzle for about a minute, just until the garlic turns pale gold and fragrant.

  2. 2

    Tip in the cherry tomatoes and smoked paprika. Cook for 6–8 minutes, pressing the tomatoes gently with a spoon, until their skins blister and burst and the pan smells deeply savoury.

    💡 Resist stirring too early — a little char on the tomato skins is where the flavour lives.

  3. 3

    Stir in the dried orzo and toast for 1 minute, coating every grain in the saffron oil. Pour in the hot vegetable stock and bring to a gentle simmer.

  4. 4

    Simmer uncovered for 10–12 minutes, stirring often, until the orzo is tender and the liquid has thickened to a glossy, risotto-like sauce. Add a splash more stock if it tightens too much.

    📝 Note: orzo keeps absorbing liquid off the heat — pull it a touch looser than you think.

  5. 5

    Off the heat, fold through the lemon zest, lemon juice and most of the basil. Season generously. Spoon into warm bowls, scatter with feta and the reserved basil, and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

  • Make it vegan: skip the feta or swap for a marinated almond ricotta.
  • Add greens: stir a few handfuls of baby spinach in with the lemon at the end.
  • Storage: keeps 3 days chilled; loosen with a splash of stock when reheating.
