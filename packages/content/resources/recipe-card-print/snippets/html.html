Roasted Tomato & Saffron Orzo

Blistered cherry tomatoes, toasted orzo, a whisper of saffron and lemon.

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Serves4
  • LevelEasy

🥕 Ingredients

  • 1½ cups orzo pasta
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes 🍅
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced 🧄
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small shallot, minced
  • 2½ cups vegetable stock
  • Zest + juice of 1 lemon 🍋
  • ¼ cup torn basil 🌿
  • Sea salt & black pepper
  • Shaved pecorino, to finish

🌿 Method

  1. Steep saffron in 2 tbsp warm stock; set aside to bloom.
  2. Roast tomatoes & garlic in oil at 220°C until blistered, ~12 min.
  3. Toast orzo with shallot until golden and nutty, 2–3 min.
  4. Add saffron stock and remaining stock; simmer, stirring, 9 min.
  5. Fold in roasted tomatoes, lemon zest & juice. Season well.
  6. Finish with basil and shaved pecorino. Serve warm.
