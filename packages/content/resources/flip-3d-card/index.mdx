## 3D Flip Card

Front and back faces share one element. Hover or focus rotates the inner wrapper `180deg` on Y.

### How it works

The scene sets `perspective`. The inner element uses `transform-style: preserve-3d` and `transition` on `transform`. The back face is pre-rotated `rotateY(180deg)`, and both faces use `backface-visibility: hidden`.

### When to use it

- Reveal extra details without a modal
- Team or product cards with a short back story
- Keyboard users: the card is a `button` so focus flips it too
