Use **Vitest** + **Testing Library** for fast unit and component tests. **Playwright** for cross-browser
E2E on critical flows (recommended default). **Cypress** if your team prefers its visual runner and
time-travel debugging. Typically: Vitest for logic, Playwright for E2E — not either/or.
