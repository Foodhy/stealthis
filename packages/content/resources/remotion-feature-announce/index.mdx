## Feature Announcement

A three-act Remotion composition designed to announce a major SaaS product feature with cinematic energy. The opening act runs for roughly the first three seconds: a full-screen radial glow bursts outward from the center of the frame (two layered blobs — a hot violet inner core and a wide indigo outer ring), then a glowing "NEW" pill badge scales in with an overshoot spring while the badge border pulses. The feature name "AI-Powered Analytics" flies in from the left on a spring curve, its second line rendered in a three-stop gradient from indigo through violet to cyan. A tagline sentence slides up from below to complete the hero panel before the whole section cross-fades out.

The second act (frames 100–215) reveals the three feature highlights in a staggered row — Real-time Insights, Custom Dashboards, and Smart Alerts — each card entering from below with a per-card accent color on its top border. A subtle card-level radial glow bleeds into the dark background, and each icon pops in with a tight overshoot spring before the label and description text settle. A "Part of Flowbase AI Suite v3.0" label fades in below the cards to reinforce brand context.

The final act (frames 215–300) brings a clean closing CTA: an animated divider line draws across the center, the Flowbase logo wordmark rises into frame, and an emerald "Available Now" pill with a pulsing dot glow confirms the launch state. The entire composition fades to black over the last 15 frames.
