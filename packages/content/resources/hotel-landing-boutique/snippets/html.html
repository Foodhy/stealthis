Our story

Built for those who prefer
to feel, not just stay.

Maison Verel occupies a 19th-century hôtel particulier on a quiet lane in the 6th arrondissement. Restored in 2019 by architect Céline Aubert, it preserves original mouldings, zinc parapets, and four centuries of layered light — while offering every modern comfort without announcement.

Twelve rooms. No two alike. A small, attentive team. The kind of place where the concierge knows the best table that isn't on any list.
