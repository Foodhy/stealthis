What’s in the box

Every part bagged, labeled, and counted twice. Spares included for the small stuff.

SRV-9G×2

Micro servos

9g metal-gear servos pre-fitted with leg cranks. These are the RoboBug’s muscles.

CTL-BUG1×1

BugBrain controller

Pre-flashed control board with 3 gait modes, obstacle sensor port, and USB-C for reprogramming later.

CHS-PLY3×6

Chassis plates

Laser-cut 3 mm plywood plates. Numbered, pre-drilled, and lightly engraved with assembly hints.

SCR-M2X8×24+4

Screws pack

M2×8 machine screws with nylon-insert nuts — plus four spares, because carpets eat screws.

SNS-IR40×1

Obstacle sensor

Clip-on infrared eye so RoboBug can dodge chair legs and cats (results with cats may vary).

MAN-EN24×1

Illustrated manual

24-page comic-style build guide with 1:1 part diagrams and a troubleshooting flowchart.

ASSEMBLY PREVIEW

Four steps to first steps

A peek at the build guide. Full manual included in the box.

STEP 01 / 04

Frame the chassis

Match plates A–C by their engraved numbers and bolt the sandwich frame together. Eight screws, one satisfying click.

⏱ 20 MIN🔩 8 SCREWS
STEP 02 / 04

Mount the servos

Drop both SRV-9G servos into their cradles and attach the six leg linkages. The cranks only fit one way — no guesswork.

⏱ 25 MIN🔩 12 SCREWS
STEP 03 / 04

Wire the BugBrain

Clip the controller onto the top plate and plug in the two servo leads and sensor cable. Keyed connectors — nothing to solder.

⏱ 15 MIN🔌 3 PLUGS
STEP 04 / 04

First walk

Load 4× AA batteries, flip the switch, and pick a gait: stroll, scuttle, or wiggle. Congratulations, robot parent.

⏱ 5 MIN🎉 100% JOY
