Our story

“We cook the garden, not the menu —
the menu just follows.”

Aitor Mendizabal · Chef & co-founder

Casa Olivar started as a thirty-cover dining room in a converted carriage house on Calle del Olivar in late 2017. Aitor had spent six years cooking in San Sebastián and a year in a friend's wood-fired bakery; Iria, the floor's quiet engine, came back to Madrid from a wine bar in Porto.

What the room had then is what it has now: one hearth, twelve tables, three people on the line, and a half-hectare garden in Aravaca that produces seventy percent of our vegetables May to October.

The menu changes weekly. The constants are bread made daily, hand-cut pasta on Wednesdays, a whole grilled fish on weekends, and a dry-aged steak that runs the year. Everything else is a conversation with the garden.

Milestones

Seven seasons in.

  1. 2017

    The lease

    Aitor signs a five-year lease on a former carriage house. The kitchen has one wood-fired hearth and no walk-in.

  2. 2019

    The garden

    We take over a neglected half-hectare in Aravaca. The first harvest is fourteen kilos of cherry tomatoes — they last six days on the menu.

  3. 2021

    The wine list

    Iria builds a 140-bottle natural-wine list across Spain, Portugal, and the Loire. Two by-the-glass producers stay on year-round.

  4. 2024

    The second sitting

    We add a 21:30 sitting Thursday–Saturday. The kitchen still closes at midnight; the bar stays open until one.

What we believe

Three things, mostly.

🌱

Garden first

The menu starts in Aravaca on Monday morning. If a thing isn't ready, it doesn't go on the menu — substitutions over forcing.

🔥

Live fire

The hearth runs olive and oak. The grill cools to coal-only by second service so the room stays calm.

🤝

Honest pricing

No platform fees on direct reservations, no minimum spend, no corkage on bottles you bring under €40 retail.
