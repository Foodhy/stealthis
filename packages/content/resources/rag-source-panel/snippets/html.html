gpt-grounded-4o query: “How does HNSW indexing speed up vector search?”

Hierarchical Navigable Small World (HNSW) graphs accelerate approximate nearest-neighbor search by organizing vectors into a layered proximity graph, where upper layers act as long-range express lanes and lower layers refine the result12.

A greedy walk starts at the sparse top layer and descends, so query time grows logarithmically rather than linearly with corpus size3. Recall is tunable through the efSearch parameter, trading a few extra hops for higher accuracy24.

In practice HNSW dominates production vector stores because its build cost is paid once and query latency stays flat as data grows into the millions5.
