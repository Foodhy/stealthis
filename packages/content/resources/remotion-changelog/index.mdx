## Changelog Video

A polished, dark-palette Remotion composition that presents a product release as a broadcast-quality motion card. The header section springs the product logo mark, wordmark, and gradient version badge into view with staggered entrance timing, followed by a date stamp that fades up beneath. A gradient divider sweeps in from the left to separate the header from the content body.

Three changelog items then slide in from the left with spring physics, each rendered as a surface card with a colored left-border accent. A compact category pill — green for New Feature, cyan for Improvement, red for Bug Fix — sits to the left of a bold title and supporting description line. The stagger timing gives the viewer space to read each entry before the next arrives. The composition closes with a CTA line and branded gradient URL animating up from below, then the entire scene fades out gently over the final half-second.

Use this composition to auto-generate release notes videos for social media, embed in in-app announcement banners, or batch-render across multiple product versions with swapped data.
