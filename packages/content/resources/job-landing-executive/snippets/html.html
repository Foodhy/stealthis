How we work

Four quiet stages, one appointment.

  1. 01

    Confidential brief

    A single partner meets the board to define the mandate, success profile and remuneration envelope under NDA.

  2. 02

    Discreet mapping

    We map the market off the record and approach incumbents personally — never through an advertised post.

  3. 03

    Assessed shortlist

    Each finalist arrives with a full dossier: assessment, referencing and benchmarking, ready for the board.

  4. 04

    Appointment & care

    We support negotiation, onboarding and a structured 100-day review to protect the placement.
