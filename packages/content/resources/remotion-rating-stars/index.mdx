## Animated Rating Stars

A centered dark card holds all the action. Five stars appear sequentially — each one springs in with a scale and rotation entrance, then transitions from gray to gold via an interpolated fill color, topped with a radial glow halo that blooms as the gold locks in. The staggered timing (14 frames between stars) gives the reveal a deliberate, satisfying cadence rather than a simultaneous flash.

Once the last star settles, the score "4.9" eases up from "0.0" using a slow spring, communicating the high rating without feeling rushed. Immediately below, the review count (2,847) ticks up in parallel, followed by "based on N reviews" fading in with a subtle upward slide. A hairline gold gradient rule then expands across the card to separate the headline rating from the breakdown section.

The distribution chart presents five rows — 5★ through 1★ — each sliding in from the left with its own stagger. A `spring()`-driven bar fills from zero to the final percentage (78 / 14 / 5 / 2 / 1 %), colored from gold down to muted slate so the dominant tier reads instantly. Percentage labels fade in after each bar completes. The composition fades out over the final 15 frames. Swap in your own product name, score, and distribution by editing the constants at the top of the file.
