## UI Showcase Reel

A 12-second design-system highlight reel that cycles through six distinct SaaS UI panels in rapid succession. Each panel enters with a spring-driven slide from the right, holds for roughly two seconds so viewers can absorb the content, then exits with a cubic ease to the left as the next panel arrives. The entrance spring (damping 18, stiffness 160) gives a confident, snappy feel without excessive bounce.

Every panel has its own staggered internal reveal: table rows slide up one by one, chart bars animate from zero height, kanban columns cascade in from top, notification items fly in from the right, and toggle switches animate on load. A bottom filmstrip persists across all panels, showing thumbnail previews of all six scenes with the active thumbnail highlighted in the panel's accent colour and a soft glow ring. The composition opens with a two-second Flowbase logo splash, then fades out over the final 15 frames.

Use this reel as a marketing video for a component library, a design-system launch announcement, or a SaaS onboarding walkthrough. Swap out the `PANELS`, data arrays, and colour tokens at the top of the file to match any product.
