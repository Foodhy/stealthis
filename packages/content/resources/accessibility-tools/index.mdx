**axe DevTools** for developers running WCAG checks in the browser and CI. **Stark** for designers
catching contrast and vision issues in Figma before handoff. **WAVE** for free, quick manual
audits. After design, use [Responsive Design Tools](/r/design-dev-tools) for multi-viewport QA.
