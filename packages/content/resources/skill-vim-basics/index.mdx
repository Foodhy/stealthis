# Vim Basics — Navigation & Editing

A practical, copy-pasteable cheatsheet for the core of Vim: the modes, the `hjkl`
navigation keys, word motions, the delete/yank/paste trio, searching, command-line
commands, undo/redo, and the operator + motion grammar that makes Vim composable.

## What this skill is

A single-page reference for the commands you reach for every day in Vim. It is
aimed at developers who are new to modal editing or who want a quick refresher of
the essential keystrokes and the grammar behind them.

## When to use it

Pull this up while learning Vim, when configuring `vi`-mode in your shell or IDE
plugin (VSCode Vim, IdeaVim, etc.), or any time you forget a motion or operator.
Practice a few lines a day and the muscle memory follows.

## Where the full cheatsheet lives

The complete reference — every command, real workflows, and gotchas — is in the
snippet at `snippets/markdown.md`. Copy it into your notes or keep it open beside
your editor.
