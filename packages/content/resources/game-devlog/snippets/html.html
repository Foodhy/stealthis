Overview

Update 1.4 is the biggest content drop since launch. We're opening the Hollow Reach, a storm-locked endgame region with three new biomes, rebuilding the Sentinel class from the frame up, and shipping the first iteration of 4-player co-op raids. The patch is live now on PC, PlayStation, and Xbox — cross-progression carries everything over.

This devlog walks through the headline features, the reasoning behind the Sentinel changes, and the full changelog. If you only have two minutes, jump to the patch highlights below.

The Hollow Reach

The Reach sits beyond the Ember Gate, sealed since the prologue. It's our densest region yet: verticality-first layout, dynamic storm cells that reroute patrol AI, and a region-wide reputation track with the Cindermark Covenant. Recommended power: 740+.

Storms aren't cosmetic. When a cell passes over a zone, enemy factions retreat to shelter routes, opening stealth paths that don't exist in clear weather — and Voltaic enemies gain a chain-arc buff, so the risk scales with the reward.

Co-op raids: Vault of Static

The first raid, Vault of Static, supports four players with role-agnostic mechanics: every encounter can be solved by any class composition, but coordination shortcuts exist for premade squads. Expect a 45–70 minute clear on your first weeks.

Sentinel class rework

The Sentinel has been over-represented in defensive play and under-represented everywhere else — 61% of Sentinel builds used the same three barrier mods. The rework keeps the class fantasy (the immovable anchor) but gives every barrier an offensive counterpart.

  • Aegis Pulse now detonates when the barrier expires, scaling with damage absorbed.
  • Bulwark Dash replaces Static Wall — a mobile shield-charge that staggers elites.
  • Barrier uptime reduced from 12s → 8s; cooldown reduced from 24s → 15s. More decisions, less camping.
