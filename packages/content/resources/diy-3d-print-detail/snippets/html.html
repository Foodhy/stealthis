Parametric Cable Organizer Clip

Snap-fit desk clip for 2–8 mm cables. Fully parametric — regenerate for any desk edge from 12 to 40 mm thick. No supports, prints in under an hour.

About this model

This clip started as a fix for the rat's nest behind my monitor arm and turned into a rabbit hole of snap-fit tuning. The jaw uses a living hinge with a 0.45 mm flex wall, so cables click in one-handed and stay put — no adhesive, no screws, no desk damage.

Everything is parametric. Open cable_clip.scad and change three numbers: desk_thickness, cable_slots, and slot_diameter. The default STL fits a 25 mm desktop with three slots (4, 6 and 8 mm).

  • ✓ Prints flat, zero supports, zero bridging over 8 mm
  • ✓ Tested to 1.8 kg pull-out force in PETG
  • ✓ Grip pads printed in TPU (optional file included)
  • ✓ Tolerances tuned for 0.4 mm nozzles — no post-processing
Remix note — v2.3 merges @spindleworks' wider jaw and fixes the hinge crack reported on cold PLA. If your first layer squishes more than 0.05 mm, scale X/Y to 100.5% for a snug desk fit.
