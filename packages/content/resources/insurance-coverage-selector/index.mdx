## Coverage Selector

A reassuring auto-insurance coverage builder. Each coverage module — **liability**, **collision**,
**comprehensive**, **roadside assistance** and **rental reimbursement** — has a toggle and, where it
applies, a coverage-limit or deductible selector. Flipping a toggle or changing a limit instantly
recalculates the running monthly premium and refreshes the summary panel on the right.

The summary lists every included coverage with its individual cost, shows a confident big number for
the monthly total, and notes the equivalent annual figure. Required coverages (liability) stay locked
on, while optional add-ons can be switched freely. All pricing math is vanilla JS — no libraries.

Calm blues and a trust-green accent, rounded cards, clear deductible chips and accessible labels and
focus styles make the choices feel safe and easy to scan.
