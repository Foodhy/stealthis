## Save the Date Animation

A clean, editorial save-the-date card rendered as a Remotion video. The composition sits at a square 1080×1080 — ideal for Instagram, WhatsApp, and printed invitations. Every element enters through a carefully sequenced spring or interpolate animation so the reveal feels handcrafted rather than mechanical.

The color palette centres on warm ivory (`#faf8f4`) with antique gold accents (`#c9a84c`), giving the piece a timeless, understated feel. Typography mixes a serif stack (Georgia) for the couple names and the large day numeral, with a light system-ui sans-serif for the "SAVE THE DATE" badge, month, year, and location — a classic pairing that reads well at any size.

Customise `NAMES`, `DATE`, and `LOCATION` at the top of the file to personalise the card. The `GOLD` and `IVORY` tokens can be adjusted to match any wedding palette.
