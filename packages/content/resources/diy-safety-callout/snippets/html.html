DANGER WARNING TIP NOTE

01 · Standard Callouts

4 LEVELS
Danger

High voltage inside the Volta-Werk PSU-900

Capacitors inside the supply hold a lethal charge for up to 15 min after unplugging. Never open the case until the bleed LED (D7) is fully dark.

  1. Unplug the unit and wait a full 15 minutes with the switch in OFF.
  2. Verify < 5 V across C3/C4 with a multimeter before touching anything.
  3. Use a 10 kΩ / 5 W bleeder resistor on insulated leads — never a screwdriver short.
Warning

Hot surface — soldering station tip reaches 380 °C

The Fluxhound FH-60 iron stays above burn temperature for ~4 min after power-off. Always return it to the spring holder — never rest it on the kraft mat.

Set the station to standby (150 °C) between joints. If you smell scorched flux, wipe the tip on brass wool — a wet sponge thermal-shocks the plating and shortens tip life.

Pro Tip

Pre-tin both wires before joining them

A thin coat of solder on each conductor means the splice flows in under 2 s — less heat into the insulation and a joint that actually holds.

Note

Kit contents vary by batch

Kits shipped after batch BB-2604 include the revised strain-relief clip (P/N 114-C). Older kits use the friction sleeve — both work with this guide.

Warning · Dismissible

Clamp the workpiece before drilling

Hand-holding sheet stock under a drill press is the #1 cause of workshop injuries in our incident log. Two clamps minimum on anything smaller than 300 mm. This notice stays hidden once dismissed (stored locally on this device).

03 · Embedded in Steps

GUIDE FLOW
  1. 04

    Route the harness through the chassis grommet

    Feed the 6-wire harness through grommet G2 and leave 80 mm of slack on the board side.

  2. 05

    Connect the battery leads

    Red to BAT+, black to BAT−. Torque the terminal screws to 0.4 N·m.

    Danger

    Reversed polarity destroys the BMS instantly and can vent the cell. Triple-check lead colors before tightening.

  3. 06

    Close the enclosure

    Seat the lid gasket and drive all six M3×8 screws in a star pattern.

    Pro Tip

    Start every screw two turns by hand — self-tappers cross-thread ABS bosses if you lead with the driver.
