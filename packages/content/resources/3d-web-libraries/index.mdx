These libraries sit **above** raw GPU APIs — see [Graphics & Rendering APIs](/r/graphics-rendering-apis) for
WebGL, WebGPU, and the like. **model-viewer** is the fastest path when you only need to show a GLB product
model or AR preview. **three.js** and **React Three Fiber** cover custom 3D apps (R3F if you are in React).
**Babylon.js** and **PlayCanvas** add full engine and team workflows. **Zdog** and **p5.js** suit light
illustrations and creative coding. **Phaser** vs **PixiJS**: framework with physics/scenes vs pure 2D renderer.
**A-Frame** for WebXR; **OGL** and **regl** for custom WebGL pipelines.
