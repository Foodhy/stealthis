The Fabric Story

We start with the fibre, not the season.

01

Undyed wool

Sourced from a single mill in northern Portugal. Left in its natural écru tone — no dye, no bleach, no microplastic shed.

02

Slow construction

Each coat passes through 31 hands over two days. Seams are double-faced, so there is no lining to fray or pucker.

03

Made to be mended

Every garment is registered to you. Send it back any time and our atelier will re-press, re-button or reweave it — free, for life.
